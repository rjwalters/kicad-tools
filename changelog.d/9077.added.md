After clipping, the `pin1_marker` check is re-run on the edited board; a footprint that had a recognised marker before the clip and none after is listed in `hole_clearance_marker_lost` (JSON) and as a text/summary warning. Exit code unchanged: clipping ink off a hole is correct, and the report tells you to re-add the marker clear of the hole.
