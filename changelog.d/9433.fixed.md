- **Silkscreen checks honour `(justify ...)` on board and footprint text** (Issue #9433). The silk text bounding box was always centred on the text anchor, so a `(justify left)` pin-name text raised phantom silk-over-copper / silk-overlap warnings that KiCad's DRC did not. `GraphicText` and `FootprintText` now record `justify`, and the box starts/ends at the anchor for `left`/`right` and hangs/stands for `top`/`bottom`, rotated about the anchor. Centred text is unchanged; `mirror` (back-side) text swaps `left`/`right`, matching KiCad's plotted output. Rotated, justified `fp_text` is now rotated at every call site.
