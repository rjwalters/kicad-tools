- **`kct export` detects jump V-scoring on third-party panels** (Issue #6193).
  Untagged collinear score pieces on a user layer that together reach both
  outline edges, with every gap crossing interior Edge.Cuts geometry (a seam or
  slot), now count as a V-score, so their layer is plotted. Dashed or partial
  documentation lines over solid board are still ignored.
