- **`kct export` detects partial and jump V-scoring on third-party panels** (Issue #6193).
  An untagged score line that runs from an outline edge to a slot or cutout
  edge (within 0.1 mm), or from edge to edge with every gap skipping a slot, a
  cutout or the space between boards, now counts as a V-score, so its layer is
  plotted. Its ends and gaps must line up with Edge.Cuts geometry, so section
  dividers, fold lines, title-block rules and dashed lines are still ignored.
  For layouts the geometry cannot prove, the new `--vscore-layer LAYER` option
  on `kct export` and `kicad-export-gerbers` (`GerberConfig.vscore_layers`)
  plots the named layer explicitly.
