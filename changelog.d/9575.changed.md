- **`kct check` evidence no longer ships raw mask/copper WKT** (Issue #9575). The
  committed `kct-check.json` / `check-report.json` serialization of a mask-copper
  assessment replaces every `geometry_provenance` `layers_wkt` map (export-level,
  per `source_geometries` entry and per `source_objects` entry) with per-layer
  `{"sha256", "bytes"}` digest records, keeping hashes, binding, measurements and
  features intact. A poured 4-layer board's report drops from ~32.1 MB to ~0.94 MB
  (34x); the in-memory assessment still carries the full geometry.
