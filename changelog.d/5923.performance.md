- **Diff-pair corridor-yield re-run is capped in proportion to the first routing pass** (Issue #5923). The one strategy re-run after a coupled pair yields its corridor now gets `clamp(1.5 x first-pass wall time, 60 s, 300 s)` instead of a fixed 300 s, so a yield that does not pay costs at most about one and a half extra main passes. `KCT_CORRIDOR_YIELD_RERUN_FACTOR` and `KCT_CORRIDOR_YIELD_RERUN_FLOOR_S` tune the derivation; an explicit `KCT_CORRIDOR_YIELD_RERUN_S` is still an absolute cap, and `KCT_CORRIDOR_YIELD=0` still disables the recovery entirely.
