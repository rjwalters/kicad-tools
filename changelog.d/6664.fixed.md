- **Lattice diff-pair routes are skew-tuned to `kct check`'s own limit**
  (Issue #6664). With `--route-engine lattice --differential-pairs`, the
  existing diff-pair skew tuner now runs automatically after routing, on every
  pair whose skew `kct check` would fail as an error
  (`resolve_pair_thresholds(...).skew_severity == "error"`), tuning to that
  same limit regardless of the class's `length_critical` flag. Pairs are
  detected with the net-class map, so class-declared (`diffpair_partner`)
  pairs are included, and one line per pair reports the reason, skew before
  and after, and the limit; a pair left over the limit is flagged
  `STILL OVER LIMIT`. Board 06b routed with the lattice engine is now
  manufacturable: its four LVDS skew errors (0.236 / 0.128 mm against a
  0.1 mm limit) are gone in both `kct check --mfr jlcpcb` and kicad-cli DRC.
  The grid engine and `--length-match-diffpairs` are unchanged.
