- **`kct route` names budget-starved nets in the routing summary** (Issue
  #9601). A net whose A* search was cut off by its wall-clock deadline or
  iteration cap used to be reported as a bare "No path found"; budget-labelled
  give-ups (`timeout`, `iteration_limit`) now print their real reason —
  explicitly leaving the geometry undetermined — and a SEARCH BUDGET remedy
  matching the cut-off: a coarser `--grid`, `--per-net-timeout` for
  wall-clock give-ups, `--per-net-iterations` / `--max-search-iterations`
  for iteration-limit give-ups, and `kct net-status --why`. Rows 5/6/9 of
  the PCBJam router audit are dispositioned in the PR; the remaining fixes
  are tracked in #9620/#9621/#9622.
