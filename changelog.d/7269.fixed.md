- **The coupled diff-pair skew trim works on tightly coupled pairs**
  (Issue #7269). When a coupled pair commits with a length mismatch, the
  router adds a serpentine to the shorter half. On a tight pair this trim
  never ran. The partner's grid clearance envelope covers the shorter
  half's own trace, so every bulge was rejected as touching a foreign net
  before it left the trunk. When it did pass, the 0.3 mm amplitude floor
  meant a short request could add several times the length needed and leave
  the skew worse. If the old comb fails or overshoots, the router now tries
  rectangular bulges sized to the mismatch and slides them along the run
  until one fits. These checks still apply: exact partner segment and via
  clearance, the grid's own clearance check against all committed copper,
  and no increase in the pair's `kct check` uncoupled length. On board 07
  (seed 42), MIPI_DAT0 skew goes from 0.624 to 0.001 mm and MIPI_CLK from
  0.668 to 0.008 mm, which clears two `diffpair_length_skew` errors.
