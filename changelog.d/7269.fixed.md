- **The coupled diff-pair skew trim can no longer raise a pair's combined
  uncoupled length** (Issue #7269). When a coupled pair commits with a
  length mismatch, the router tries to trim the skew. A trim is now
  accepted only if it reduces the skew and leaves the pair's combined
  P plus N uncoupled length (the KiCad-equivalent figure #6858 is judged
  in) no higher than before. Otherwise the pair keeps its original copper.
  Two fixes come with the gate. On a tight pair, the partner's grid
  clearance envelope no longer rejects every bulge on the shorter half
  before it leaves the trunk. The legacy comb can no longer overshoot a
  short request and leave the skew worse. If no bulge on the shorter half
  passes the gate, the router tries straightening a detour on the longer
  half, under the same clearance checks. A bulge's legs are never coupled,
  so on pairs that are coupled end to end (board 07 and board 06rf MIPI)
  the gated trim leaves the pair unchanged, as it was before this fix.
