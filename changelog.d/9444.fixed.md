- **`mask_to_copper` excuses holes standing mid-run on a reinforce strip** (Issue #9444). A same-net layer-change `via`, or a `gr_circle` hole no wider than the record's anchor drill plus flash (with its mask circles on both sides), whose **centre** lies on a recorded run's strip is now attributed to the run, so its own copper reads `within_reinforce_run` instead of a same-net error. A hole merely beside the strip, an oversize circle, a different-net via, and different-net copper in their clearance band keep their verdict.
