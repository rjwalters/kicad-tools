- **`kct check` can newly fail on JLCPCB boards** (Issue #8844). `silk_pad_clearance`
  now reports an `error` at <= 0.12 mm silk-to-pad (and a `warning` at <= 0.18 mm),
  and the new `silk_hole_overlap` rule warns on silkscreen over via/PTH/NPTH drill
  holes, including tented vias. Move or trim the flagged silk, or waive the finding.
  The bands also relax one case: on JLCPCB profiles a silk-to-pad gap between
  0.12 mm and 0.15 mm was an `error` under the old single 0.15 mm floor and is now
  a `warning`, so a gate that counted it as fab-blocking no longer does.
