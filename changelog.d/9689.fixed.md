- **`kct check` measures Edge.Cuts arcs, not their two chords** (Issue #9689). `get_board_outline_segments` replaced every Edge.Cuts arc — board-level `gr_arc` and footprint `fp_arc` alike — with the two chords start→mid and mid→end, ignoring the arc's sagitta (about `0.29 × r` for a 180° arc: 3.5 mm at r = 12 mm, far beyond any 0.5 mm edge clearance). `EdgeClearanceRule` therefore missed copper inside an arc's bulge that kicad-cli flags, and flagged copper clearing the true arc. Arcs now reach the rule through the certified bounded-error tessellator (10 nm Hausdorff bound, preserved endpoints, midpoint and sweep), and the rule charges that certified bound against measured clearances on traces, vias, pads and zone vertices, keeping the existing 0.1 µm straight-edge tolerance exact for straight outlines. Curved outlines also become slightly denser: cubic tessellation in the same path now uses the same 10 nm bound instead of its 0.1 µm default, and circle rings report their certified sagitta. Other consumers of the segment list (creepage obstacles, connector access, silkscreen and placement distances, board scripts) inherit the denser chain unchanged — the list stays a plain iterable of board-relative endpoint pairs, now carrying its certified error as `max_error_mm` for distance-measuring callers.
