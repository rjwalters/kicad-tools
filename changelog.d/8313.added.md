- **`kct pcb prune --current-paths FILE [--wide MM]` breaks loops on declared power nets** (Issue #8313). On the nets named in the current-path specs, each copper loop, including loops closed through vias, is broken by removing its narrowest segment below `--wide` (default 0.5 mm) and any stub that cut leaves dead-ended, so `current-paths-audit` stops calling the run ambiguous and `pcb reinforce` accepts it. Pads and zones are never disconnected; loops whose segments are all at or above `--wide` are left in place and listed (`uncut_loops` in `--format json`).
