- **`kct parts search` no longer reads resistance nominals off non-resistor
  parts** (Issue #9551). LED colour temperatures (`6500K`), CCT ranges
  (`13172K~100000K`), MOSFET `Rds(on)` figures (`47mΩ@1.8V`) and crystal
  MPN/ESR tokens no longer parse as ohms: a `6.5M` query cannot rank an LED
  above real 6.5 MΩ resistors, and crystal pages suppress the "no result has
  the requested resistance" note. `RESISTOR` and `OTHER` categories parse as
  before, so real resistors with heuristic-uncategorised descriptions are
  unaffected.
