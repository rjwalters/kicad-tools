- **Copper-clearance findings in the 100-500 nm band now pass `kct check`** (Issue #9619). The probed copper-clearance rules (`clearance`, `diffpair_clearance_intra`, `netclass_clearance_copper`) that used to fail for shortfalls between 0.1 um and 0.5 um under the requirement now pass, matching pinned KiCad 10.0.6. If you relied on kct flagging that band (e.g. a fab whose DRC is stricter than KiCad's), re-check your boards against the fab's own rules. All other checks are unchanged: dimension, annular-ring, netclass-floor, hole, silk, via-in-pad and creepage verdicts keep their exact pre-#9619 thresholds (pinned kicad-cli flags a 100 nm track-width or drill shortfall outright, so those checks stay at 0.1 um), and no written copper changes -- the router, `kct pcb move-via`, `kct fix-vias` and the placement optimiser keep their 100 nm margin (`WRITER_CLEARANCE_MARGIN_MM`), so emitted copper is no tighter than before.
