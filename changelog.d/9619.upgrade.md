- **Clearance findings in the 100-500 nm band now pass `kct check`** (Issue #9619). Checks that used to fail for shortfalls between 0.1 um and 0.5 um under the requirement now pass, matching pinned KiCad 10.0.6. If you relied on kct flagging that band (e.g. a fab whose DRC is stricter than KiCad's), re-check your boards against the fab's own rules. No written copper changes: the router, `kct pcb move-via`, `kct fix-vias` and the placement optimiser keep their 100 nm margin (`WRITER_CLEARANCE_MARGIN_MM`), so emitted copper is no tighter than before, and creepage verdicts are unchanged.
