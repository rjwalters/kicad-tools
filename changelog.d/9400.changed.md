- **`kct fix-drc --only copper-gap` documents its trace-length side effect**
  (Issue #9400). A copper-gap via move drags the same-net track ends anchored
  at the via, so the length of the attached tracks changes with it and a net
  under a length or skew constraint (a differential pair or match group
  sitting at its limit) can flip a timing check even though the slit itself is
  repaired. The per-move validation checks clearance and hole-to-hole spacing
  only and cannot see a pair-level property such as skew; the CLI reference
  now says so in the `copper-gap` paragraph.
