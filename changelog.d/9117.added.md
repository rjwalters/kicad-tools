- **`TraceLengthAnalyzer` measures the physical pad-to-pad copper path**
  (Issue #9117). New `measure_net_path` / `with_path_length` on
  `TraceLengthAnalyzer` compute, per net, the shortest copper path between
  pads through the shared copper-contact graph plus the off-path copper
  length (dead-end loops, overlapping duplicates, stubs) as new
  `TraceLengthReport` fields; summed `total_length_mm` keeps its meaning.
  Board 07's `validate.py` now builds match-group lengths from that path
  and the vias it runs through, and refuses matched nets carrying more
  than 2 mm of off-path copper.
