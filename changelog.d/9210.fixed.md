- **Fold-back repair no longer declines a legal repair because of copper a
  later repair removed** (Issue #9210). `plan_foldback_repairs` kept every
  segment a repair added in its cross-net clearance veto list, including chords
  a later repair in the same `(net, layer)` group superseded. Those segments are
  absent from the applied copper, so they could veto a later group's repair and
  inflate `FoldBackResult.skipped`. Superseded segments are now dropped from the
  list; surviving chords still veto.
