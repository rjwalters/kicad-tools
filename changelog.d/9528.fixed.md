- **`kct check` no longer reports copper-less custom pads as unmeasured copper** (Issue #9528). A pad
  with no `*.Cu` layer (e.g. the stock library's paste-only custom pads) is skipped by `clearance`,
  `tht_smd_spacing` and `physical_copper_gap`, and by `silk_over_copper` when it also has no mask
  layer, instead of raising the blocking `physical_copper_gap_incomplete` error. Custom pads that do
  carry copper but whose outline cannot be resolved are still reported incomplete.
