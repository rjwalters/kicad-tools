- **Lattice router judges pad clearance against the true pad outline** (Issue #7503). Via, track and differential-pair leg keep-outs beside rounded, oval, circular or rotated pads now follow the real copper (via the clearance kernel) instead of an inflated bounding square, so legal sites diagonal to a pad corner are no longer rejected. The keep-out reach includes the pad's net-class clearance, and unknown corner radii stay rectangular, so the check is never looser than the DRC engines. Differential-pair gateway fan legs that would run side by side inside the impedance rule's 0.5 mm coupling window at a gap other than the pair's (two diagonal legs from a 1.0 mm USB-C row, about 112 ohm against 90) are now declined, so the pad-to-via escape no longer leaves wrong-gap coupled copper.
