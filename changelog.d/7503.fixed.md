- **Lattice router judges pad clearance against the true pad outline** (Issue #7503). Via and track keep-outs beside rounded, oval, circular or rotated pads now follow the real copper (via the clearance kernel) instead of an inflated bounding square, so legal sites diagonal to a pad corner are no longer rejected. Unknown corner radii stay rectangular, so the check is never looser than the DRC engines. Boards 02-07 and the diffpair benches were not re-run for this change.
