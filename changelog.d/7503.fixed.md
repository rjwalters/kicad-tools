- **Lattice router judges pad clearance against the true pad outline** (Issue #7503). Via, track and differential-pair leg keep-outs beside rounded, oval, circular or rotated pads now follow the real copper (via the clearance kernel) instead of an inflated bounding square, so legal sites diagonal to a pad corner are no longer rejected. The keep-out reach includes the pad's net-class clearance, and unknown corner radii stay rectangular, so the check is never looser than the DRC engines.
