- **`mask_to_copper` errors can drop to info for spoke-connected own pours**
  (Issue #9284). Consumers that read
  `mask_copper_assessments[].measurements[].disposition` must accept the new
  value `own_pour_thermal`; the assessment can pass with it. Error counts fall on
  boards whose pads join their own pour through thermal spokes with the pour
  body at least 0.15 mm from the opening (JLCPCB policy); warning counts are
  unchanged and no finding becomes more severe. The native mask worker now also
  reports each pad's and zone's declared zone connection.
