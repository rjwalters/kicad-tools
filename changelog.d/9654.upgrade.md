- **`kct check` can newly report `path_ampacity` "too narrow" errors on
  fully wired buttress legs** (Issue #9654). The hole-to-pad stub at each end
  of a wire leg (~3 mm on a default `kct pcb reinforce` placement) is bare
  copper and is now judged at its own width instead of riding the wire's
  credit, so a board whose wire previously silenced an undersized trace fails
  again on the stubs. Widen the copper under the stubs (the wire still covers
  the span between the anchors), or move the anchors or pads so the wire
  covers the stretch.
