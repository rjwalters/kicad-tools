- **Lattice routing length-balances crossing USB-C row-to-row ties** (Issue #7869). On a single-layer mirrored USB-C receptacle the two same-net ties of a pair cross. The lattice no longer declines both and lattice-routes the extras. It now plans them as one set: the blocked net goes round its partner's pad, and the partner is padded to the same length with plain 45-degree-chamfered copper. Board 06rf `USB2_D` goes from 2.127 mm skew (limit 1.0 mm) to 0.02 mm on seeds 42 and 43 in both `kct check` and pinned kicad-cli. No skew limit, tuner bound or check rule changed, and the ties add no vias.
