- **Silk hole tools no longer treat blind/buried via drills as piercing both
  silk sides** (Issue #9076). `iter_drill_holes` now reports the silk sides a
  drill actually reaches -- a blind or micro via counts only on its outer
  side, a buried via on neither, through vias and pad holes on both -- so
  `silk_hole_overlap`, `kct place-silk-refs` and
  `kct fix-silkscreen --clip-to-holes` stop flagging or clearing far-side
  silk that no drill can touch.
