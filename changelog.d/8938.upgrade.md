- **Boards that passed `kct check --mfr jlcpcb` can now fail** (Issue #8938). Same-net copper gaps below the fab's danger edge — e.g. a via sitting at a pad edge — are errors, not warnings, so a previously "0 errors" board can newly report errors and fail `kct check`, `kct readiness` and CI's `routed-pcb-drc-check` gate. Waive individual findings in `.kct_waivers.json` (`"rule": "physical_copper_gap"` plus the finding's `nets` or `items`) with a `reason` and `issue`; waived same-net findings are listed and counted in the output.
