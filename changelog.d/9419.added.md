- **`kct pcb reinforce BOARD --adopt` records wires already on the board** (Issue #9419). Reads each solder-mask strip between two same-net anchor holes of a wire gauge's drill back into `<board>.reinforcement.json` without moving copper or writing the board, so a hand-adjusted wire can be recorded for `mask_to_copper` (#9315). Wires already in the sidecar are kept; strips that cannot be resolved are reported, not guessed. `--net` restricts it, `--dry-run` previews.
- **`mask_to_copper` excuses a recorded run's far-side openings and same-net copper under its strip** (Issue #9419). The opposite mask side's anchor flashes and end holes attribute to the run, and same-net track, via or pad exposed there or under the run's strip (tee branch, adjoining wire's anchor, layer-change via, doubled segment) measures `within_reinforce_run`. Different-net copper stays an error.
