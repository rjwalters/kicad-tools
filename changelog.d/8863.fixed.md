- **Silk circles and arcs now reach the silk checks** (Issue #8863). `fp_circle`/`gr_circle`
  and `fp_arc`/`gr_arc` on silkscreen layers contribute geometry to `silk_over_copper`,
  `silk_overlap`, `silk_edge_clearance` and `silk_pad_clearance`, so a ring or arc across a
  pad is now reported. `silk_geometry_unmodeled` no longer fires for them. Arcs joined
  end-to-end to a line or arc of the same footprint are not flagged as overlapping.
