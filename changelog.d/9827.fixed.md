- **`kct sch pin-map` no longer joins overlapping collinear wires, and no longer reuses `_local_N` names across sheets** (Issue #9827). Two wires on one line that overlap, with no shared endpoint and no junction on the overlap, were treated as one net. A pin at one wire's end that lay inside the other wire was joined to it too. This put a CPVDD pin on the digital `+3.3V` rail and merged two LED-cathode nets. kicad-cli keeps such wires on separate nets, and pin-map now does the same. A junction or label on the overlap still joins them. Unnamed nets are also numbered across the whole hierarchy now: before, every sheet started again at `_local_1`, so unconnected nets on different sheets got the same name. The wire-graph fix also applies to the connection checks that share it (`sch_check_connections`, `sch_find_unconnected`).
