- **`kct route --order-method regret`** (Issue #9001). New net-order method:
  ranks nets by regret -- cost(second-best) minus cost(best) from two
  empty-board A* probes per net -- and commits the biggest-loss nets first,
  descending within each net-class priority band. A net with only one
  corridor sorts first in its band; a failed probe leaves the net at its
  default position. Accepted on every routing path (per attempt, like
  `crossing`), opt-in, off by default; the `Net order:` log line reports the
  probe A*-call count so the pre-pass cost is readable next to the harness's
  load-independent `astar_calls` column.
