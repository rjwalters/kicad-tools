- **`kct check` flags copper inside footprint-local filled Edge.Cuts cut-outs** (Issue #9692). An `fp_circle`/`fp_poly`/`fp_rect` on Edge.Cuts inside a footprint resolves to a filled cut-out under the same KiCad rules as its board-level sibling (explicit `(fill yes)`/`(fill solid)` token; or no fill token with a zero effective stroke width for circle/rect, never for poly), and kicad-cli's `copper_edge_clearance` reports copper inside it with actual 0.0 mm. kct read only the stroke geometry the footprint outline reader re-emits, so a deep-interior track over a filled footprint shape passed (a `fill yes` `fp_poly` over a 3 mm probe grid: kicad-cli flagged 146 tracks, kct 24). The edge-clearance rule now also consumes footprint-local filled regions in the footprint's rotated board frame, so traces, vias, pads and zone vertices inside one report gap 0; `(fill no)` shapes keep their stroke-only reading (native parity pinned against the pinned kicad-cli 10.0.6 image, rotated footprints included).
