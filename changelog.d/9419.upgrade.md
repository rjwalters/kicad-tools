- **`mask_to_copper` error counts drop on boards with a reinforcement sidecar** (Issue #9419). Same-net copper under a recorded wire's strip, and same-net copper exposed by the far-side anchor/end-hole openings, is now `within_reinforce_run` (info) instead of an error. Boards whose wires were adjusted by hand can run `kct pcb reinforce BOARD --adopt` once to create the sidecar.
