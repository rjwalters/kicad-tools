- **Items files that relied on silently ignored fields now fail to parse**
  (Issue #9618). Payloads that previously passed validation with fields
  dropped — a `segment` with `mid`, a `via` with `layer` or `width` — now
  raise `ItemsFileError` and exit 1. Remove the stray field, or make the
  item its real type: `"type": "arc"` keeps the midpoint and writes a true
  `(arc ...)` node.
