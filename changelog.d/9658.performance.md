- **`kct board-metrics` corner counting scales linearly with track count** (Issue #9658). The
  same-net junction search behind the `corners` layout-quality metric compared every segment
  pair within a (net, layer) group, so a serpentine with 4,000 same-net segments spent ~3.3 s
  in it and the cost quadrupled per doubling; endpoints are now bucketed on a 0.01 mm lattice
  (matching the junction tolerance), cutting that case to ~0.05 s with a ~2x-per-doubling
  curve. Published corner counts are unchanged — verified identical on every fleet board.
