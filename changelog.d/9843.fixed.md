- **`kct zones` accepts trailing `-q/--quiet` on every verb** (Issue #9843).
  `add`, `list`, `batch`, `hv-keepout` and `fill` now advertise a local
  `-q/--quiet` after the board path, matching the spelling `zones relieve`
  already accepts. The four modifying verbs forward it to the handler exactly
  like the leading global flag (one `--quiet` when either position — or both —
  is given); `zones list` accepts it for flag-surface consistency and still
  prints the full requested text or JSON listing.
