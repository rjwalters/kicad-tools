- **Lattice hybrid diff-pair legs look for equal via counts** (Issue #7093).
  When the two single-ended legs of a #6446 hybrid (coupled trunk plus
  per-net legs) change layers a different number of times, each leg is now
  re-searched on its partner's layer sequence (the mirrored search the
  uncoupled fallback already used). A via-equal result replaces the pick only
  when it is no worse on both skew and length. Board 06rf's USB3_RX2 hybrid
  goes from 2/0 to 2/2 vias, with legs of 12.3/13.7 mm instead of 12.3/19.2
  mm. Board 07rf's TMDS_D2 stays at 0/2: its only via-equal alternative adds
  0.58 mm of skew. The grid engine is unchanged.
