- **Action: `kct check --mfr jlcpcb` can newly report `tht_smd_spacing` errors for vias** (Issue #8914). A via drilled 1.0 mm or more within 2.0 mm (copper edge to copper edge) of an SMD pad on a layer its barrel reaches is now an `error`, and 2.0-2.7 mm a `warning` -- the same bands through-hole component pads have had since #8843. Boards that use large plated holes as wire anchors or mounting points near SMD pads are the ones affected; every via on every board in this repository drills 0.15-0.3 mm and none of their counts change. Move the anchor hole, move the pad, or pass `via_drill_mm=math.inf` to `check_tht_smd_spacing` / `DRCChecker.check_tht_smd_spacing` to restore the old vias-never-judged behaviour. The `include_vias` keyword is gone; `via_drill_mm=0` is the equivalent of the old `include_vias=True`.
