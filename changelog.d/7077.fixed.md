- **`kct drc` says when schematic parity was not checked** (Issue #7077).
  When kicad-cli cannot fetch the schematic netlist (no schematic, or one that
  is not fully annotated) it skips the `--schematic-parity` checks yet exits 0
  with an empty `schematic_parity` list, so the board read as parity-clean.
  `kct drc` on a `.kicad_pcb` now prints a "Schematic parity NOT checked"
  notice in table, summary and `--mfr` output, appends
  "(schematic parity NOT checked)" to the verdict line, and adds
  `schematic_parity_checked: true|false` to `--format json`. kicad-cli is now
  located once via the shared `find_kicad_cli()` helper.
