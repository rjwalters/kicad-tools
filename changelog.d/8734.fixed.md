- **`kct pcb prune` clears `redundant_copper_covered` findings for near-collinear tails and thin loops** (Issue #8734).
  Prune and the check now share one `COVERED_TOL_MM` (1 µm; prune used 1 nm), and prune
  removes a thin same-layer loop path whose copper lies under the other path's, whatever
  sits at the loop's far end (pad, via or track joint). Findings of other shapes can still
  remain after a prune. The finding now reads "lies within the copper of a longer segment".
