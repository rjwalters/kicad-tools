- **A pair near its uncoupled-length limit can newly fail `kct check`** (Issue #6554). kct's uncoupled figure is now KiCad's, which is larger than the old projection-overlap figure on pairs with stubs at pads or with copper between the traces (board 03 `USB_D` reads 10.405 mm, up from 8.130 mm). Pairs within a few millimetres of their limit that passed before may now fail, as they already did in kicad-cli: the board 06 synthetic routing witness that CI regenerates now reports `USB2_D` at 14.194 mm against its 12.7 mm USB2_HS limit (12.519 mm before; kicad-cli 14.194 mm), recorded in that witness's historical pair baseline. No committed board is affected and no limit changed.
