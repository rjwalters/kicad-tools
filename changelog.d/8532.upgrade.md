- **New assembly-affecting warnings gate `--assembly` tapeout** (Issue #8532).
  `fiducials_missing` and `board_id_missing` are in `ASSEMBLY_AFFECTING_RULES`, so
  tapeout refuses `--assembly` until the board has them (`kct pcb add-fiducials`,
  `kct pcb add-board-info`) or they are acknowledged via `--ack-warnings`.
  `kct readiness --generate` now modifies the candidate board unless
  `--skip-fiducials` / `--skip-board-info` is passed.
