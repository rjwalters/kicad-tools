- **`kct route` costs and reports plane-zone crossings; `--protect-plane-zones` refuses splits** (Issue #9597). Pour zones on declared `PLANE` layers are now marked on the routing grid (both backends), so routing across another net's plane zone pays a per-cell cost (`cost_zone_foreign_plane`, default 10.0) instead of sailing through it blind, and every committed crossing is reported after routing ("net 'SIG' crosses zone +3.3V on In2.Cu"). The new `--protect-plane-zones` flag hard-refuses any route commit — including the trace-optimizer's post-route straightening — that would split a zone into more pad-bearing pieces than before, naming the zone. Piece counting is the `plane_split` check rule's implementation (`zone_pieces`), lifted to a shared helper rather than duplicated.
