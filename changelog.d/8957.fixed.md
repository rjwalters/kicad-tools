- **`kct stitch --avoid-pad-overlap` no longer seats stitch vias in the same-net pad slit band** (Issue #8957). The drill was kept out of same-net SMD pads but nothing constrained the copper annulus, leaving ~0.09 mm notches of air between a via's annulus and the pad it escapes — fab-view `physical_copper_gap` errors regardless of nets under the #8938 grading. With `--mfr`, candidates whose annulus-to-same-net-pad gap falls in `(0, GapBand.error_edge_mm)` of the stackup's `pad_spacing` entry (0.15 mm on jlcpcb `4layer_1oz`, 0.13 mm on `4layer_2oz`) are rejected and the ladder keeps walking; annulus overlapping the pad (a copper union) and gaps at or beyond the edge stay acceptable. Vias no acceptable strategy can seat are dropped with an explicit `same_net_pad_gap` skip reason and counted in `StitchResult.same_net_pad_gap_filtered` / the stitch JSON's `same_net_pad_gap_filtered`.
