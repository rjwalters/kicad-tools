- **`kct check` and the router measure `gr_circle` and stroked `gr_poly` custom-pad copper instead of refusing it** (Issue #9501). `custom_pad_copper` now builds the real copper of `gr_circle` primitives (a filled disc grown to `radius + width/2`; an unfilled ring between `radius +/- width/2`) and of stroked `gr_poly` outlines (the polygon buffered by half the stroke width with round joins, on top of a fill or alone around an unfilled outline), replacing the "unsupported primitive 'gr_circle'" / "unsupported gr_poly stroke width" refusals. Area matches the pinned KiCad image's own `PAD::GetEffectivePolygon` to within 1e-4 mm^2 at 0/90/180/30 degrees, front and back. On the stock library that converts ~373 `gr_circle` pads (216 footprints, e.g. `Mounting_Wuerth` spacers) and ~407 stroked-`gr_poly` pads from `*_incomplete` coverage findings to exact copper in `clearance`, `silkscreen`, `tht_smd_spacing`, `physical_copper_gap` and routing fixed-copper. `gr_rect`, `gr_arc`, `gr_line` and `gr_curve` primitives still refuse by name, and the router/DSN loaders keep degrading or refusing them exactly as before.
