- **`merge_voltage_scenarios` refuses a net spelled two ways in one scenario**
  (Issue #9665). A scenario map carrying both `/VCC` and `VCC` previously kept
  only whichever spelling came last in insertion order, silently dropping the
  other declared potential from the merged envelope. The merge now raises
  `ValueError` naming both authored spellings — matching every other
  malformed-input path in the function. Spellings may still differ *across*
  scenarios; only collisions within one map are refused.
