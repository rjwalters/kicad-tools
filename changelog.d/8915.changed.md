- **`kct pcb reinforce` skips buttress wires shorter than 15 mm by default**
  (Issue #8915). New `--min-wire-length MM` on `kct pcb reinforce`, default
  **15.0** in `ends` mode: a buttress wire needs a through-hole at each end,
  and each hole needs both outer layers and the inner planes clear, so the two
  rarely fit on a shorter link and the owner wants no wire there at all. A leg
  below the minimum is reported `unanchored` — with a reason naming the 15 mm
  minimum, in both the text report and `--format json` — and gets no anchors,
  no solder-mask opening, and no entry in the `<board>.reinforcement.json`
  wire list. `--min-wire-length 0` restores the previous behaviour.
  `--min-run-length` is now an accepted alias for the same value (existing
  invocations keep working); if both flags are given, the larger wins.
  `--anchor-mode spaced` takes no default minimum, so its anchor output is
  unchanged. The JSON report gains `min_wire_length_mm` (what was asked for)
  and `min_wire_length_effective_mm` (what was enforced).
