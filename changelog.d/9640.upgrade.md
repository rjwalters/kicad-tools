- **`kct optimize-traces` now skips nets that own a locked track** (Issue #9640). A locked track marks a run as shaped on purpose; boards that relied on the optimiser rewriting a net with a locked segment will see that net unchanged and listed under `locked_nets_skipped`. With `--voltage-map`, more nets can also be rolled back than before (a deeper shortfall on an already-failing pair now counts as a regression).
