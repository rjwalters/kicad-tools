- **`kct optimize-traces --drc-aware` now referees inner layers at the board's
  authored clearance instead of the fab preset's inner floor** (Issue #9113).
  With a fab preset whose inner clearance floor exceeds the board's authored
  clearance (e.g. jlcpcb 4-layer/2oz, inner 0.1016 mm, board authored 0.1 mm),
  the referee now scales the inner floor down to the authored clearance
  alongside the outer one, so the inner layers are refereed at the rule the
  board declares rather than at the fab preset. Relatedly, `DesignRules` now
  raises `ValueError` on construction -- including via `dataclasses.replace` --
  when the inner floor exceeds the outer floor, so downstream code that
  hand-builds a profile with that inversion now fails at construction.
