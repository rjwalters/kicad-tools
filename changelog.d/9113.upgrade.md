- **`kct optimize-traces --drc-aware` no longer crashes on tightly-authored multi-layer boards** (Issue #9113). With a fab preset whose inner clearance floor exceeds the board's authored clearance (e.g. jlcpcb 4-layer/2oz, inner 0.1016 mm, board authored 0.1 mm), the referee's `DesignRules` update raised `ValueError` and aborted the optimization. The referee now scales the inner floor down to the authored clearance alongside the outer one, so the inner layers are refereed at the rule the board declares rather than at the fab preset.
