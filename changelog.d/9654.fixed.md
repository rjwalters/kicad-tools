- **`path_ampacity` judges the unwired hole-to-pad stub of a wired leg at its
  copper width** (Issue #9654). A buttress wire is soldered between its two
  anchor holes, but the credit added in #9648 covered the whole leg polyline,
  including the short run from the component pad to the first anchor. The rule
  now splits a segment lying on a wired leg at the anchors: only the stretch
  between them gets the wire's cross-section, and the bare stubs at each end
  are judged — and reported, at their own location and width — like any other
  uncovered copper ("width 2.000mm; this stretch is not covered by a wire leg").
  Fusing is judged per stretch the same way. Legs without a recorded anchor
  range and boards without wired legs check exactly as before.
