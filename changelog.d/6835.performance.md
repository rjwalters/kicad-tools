- **Flag-off coupled diff-pair search gets a 700,000-iteration budget**
  (Issue #6835). When no per-pair budget is configured, the coupled search
  now gets 700,000 iterations per pair instead of 40,000. The corridor and
  open phases share that total, so each phase is guaranteed 350,000. The
  previous budget was below what Board07's pairs need after #6826. With the
  new budget, 6 of the 7 Board07 `--differential-pairs` searches find a
  coupled path (1 before): MIPI_CLK, MIPI_DAT0, MIPI_DAT1 and TMDS_D0/D1/D2.
  DQS does not, at any budget. Five of those six coupled routes are still
  rejected for P/N overlap and fall back to independent routing, so the
  routed board does not change. Explicit budgets (board configs, the re-route
  gate) are unaffected.
