- **`tht_smd_spacing` thresholds now match the fab's observed limits** (Issue #9421). A THT pad or anchor via closer than 2.032 mm (was 2.0 mm) to an SMD pad is an `error`, and closer than 3.05 mm (was 2.7 mm) a `warning`; JLCDFM rated 2.00 / 2.025 mm pairs as danger that the old floor passed. The fab parity table also records the fab's silk-to-hole, via-to-pad, pad-to-line and mask-opening values with source and date (via-to-pad stays ungated).
