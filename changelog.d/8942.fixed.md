- **`kct`'s removed-pad scan resolves project-local footprint libraries**
  (Issue #8942). The scan that seeds stranded-copper findings for a changed
  footprint loaded its replacement through KiCad's global libraries only, so a
  footprint declared in the project's `fp-lib-table` (including
  `${KIPRJMOD}` URIs) skipped the scan with just a log line. The replacement
  is now resolved through the project table first, then the global one.
