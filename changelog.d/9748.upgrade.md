- **`kct optimize-traces --drc-aware --mfr jlcpcb` reports mask screening and can roll back more nets** (Issue #9748). The in-process referee's `DRC errors:` totals now include a conservative `mask_to_copper` screen on JLCPCB-family runs, so before/after counts are higher than in previous releases on boards that already carry mask exposure, and the summary suffix is `incl. mask_to_copper screen` / `mask_to_copper not evaluated` instead of a bare `no regressions` — scripts matching the old suffix exactly need updating. Nets whose rewrites introduce mask exposure are newly rolled back, and the pad-escape guard newly vetoes off-axis pad entries and copper inside the 0.09 mm mask-clearance band of a pad opening; both only keep the input geometry. Manufacturers without a cited mask policy are unchanged.
