- **Router steers non-local trunks out from under fine-pitch IC bodies**
  (Issue #9622). New soft under-body surcharge: an A* step inside the body
  bbox of a fine-pitch component (pin pitch <= 0.8mm, >= 4 pads) on an
  outer layer costs `rules.cost_under_body` (default 2.0) extra
  `cost_straight` units for every net with no pad on that component, on
  both the C++ and pure-Python engines. Nets with a pad on the part are
  exempt, and the cost is additive -- a board whose only corridor runs
  under the body still routes.
