- **Doubled current-path copper no longer reads as `ambiguous`** (Issue #8790). A run deliberately duplicated on two layers between the same two junctions (including a shorter copy tied by end vias) now resolves as a named parallel group, and `path_ampacity` judges it on the summed cross-section instead of erroring. Same-layer detours, loops through another load, meshes and via-ladders stay `ambiguous`.
