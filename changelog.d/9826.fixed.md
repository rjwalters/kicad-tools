- **Plane-zone crossing cost survives rebuilt grids on both backends** (Issue #9826).
  A C++ router built after load (relaxed-clearance retry, fine-grid pass,
  diff-pair pre-phase) now copies the plane-zone marks, and a Monte Carlo /
  evolutionary trial reset re-marks them, so foreign `PLANE` zones keep their
  `cost_zone_foreign_plane` instead of being crossed for free. The C++ search
  now charges that cost only on `PLANE` layers and blocks a foreign zone on any
  other layer, matching the Python router, and its zone clear honours the layer
  and resets the net on former zone cells.
