- **`kct creepage` now treats net-0 copper as floating conductors** (Issue #8548). A netless pad or trace between an HV net and another net can shorten the creepage/clearance path (HV → island → other); the census now measures that single hop, names the island in a new `via_floating` witness key on the affected row, and reports `netless_items` / `netless_treatment` at report level. Net-0 zone fills are not collected and so are not modelled.
