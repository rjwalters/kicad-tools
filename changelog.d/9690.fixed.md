- **`kct check` flags copper inside filled Edge.Cuts cut-outs** (Issue #9690). A filled `gr_circle`/`gr_poly`/`gr_rect` on Edge.Cuts (a solid `(fill ...)` token, or — for circle/rect — no fill token with a zero stroke width, KiCad's legacy default) is a cut-out region, and kicad-cli's `copper_edge_clearance` reports copper anywhere inside it with actual 0.0 mm. kct read only stroke geometry, so a deep-interior track over such a shape passed. The edge-clearance rule now treats a trace (start/end/midpoint), via, pad or zone vertex inside a filled region as gap 0; explicitly unfilled shapes (`(fill no)`/`(fill none)`) keep their stroke-only reading, and a `gr_poly` with no fill token is never filled (all black-box probed on the pinned kicad-cli 10.0.6 image).
