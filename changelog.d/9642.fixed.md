- **`silk_pad_clearance` compares board-level silk in the pads' frame** (Issue #9642). Board-level `gr_line` / `gr_arc` (and other `gr_*` shapes) on boards whose origin is not (0, 0) were compared against board-relative pads at their raw file coordinates, producing false errors in open board and missing real overlaps. The rule now shifts them by the board origin, and reports their location in the same frame as `silk_overlap`. The pin-1 marker rule's board-level silk and the `silkscreen_line_width` / silk-coverage locations got the same shift.
