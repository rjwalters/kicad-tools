- **Escape-router via placement uses rotation-aware pad boxes** (Issue #9236). `EscapeRouter._can_place_via` and `_via_clears_other_pads` built pad boxes from raw `width`/`height`, under-sizing pads at non-cardinal angles, so a via could land in the same-net slit band or too close to a rotated foreign pad. They now use `pad_half_extents`; axis-aligned pads are unchanged.
