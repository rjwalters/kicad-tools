- **`kct check --mfr pcbway` accepts blind, buried and micro vias on 4- and
  6-layer boards** (Issue #6640). The PCBWay profile now sets
  `blind_buried_vias_supported` and `micro_vias_supported` on its 4- and
  6-layer configs, citing PCBWay's published HDI capability page (verified
  2026-10-14), so `via_type_unsupported` no longer fires there. The 2-layer
  configs and every other shipped profile keep the fail-closed `false` default.
