- **`kct check` `pad_grid` can newly warn on parts that were previously hidden by the board-wide percentile** (Issue #9434). Each footprint is now held to a tolerance derived from its own pad lattice (0.05 mm floor, 0.15 mm cap), so e.g. a lone fiducial placed 0.07 mm off the grid is flagged even when the rest of the board is clean. Findings are warnings; fix the placement, or pass `--pad-grid-tolerance MM` to pin a value.
