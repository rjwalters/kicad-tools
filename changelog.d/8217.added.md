- **`kct optimize-traces` shortens long "tent" detours** (Issue #8217). New
  `shorten_detour_pass` replaces a whole chain of 3+ segments with a straight or
  single-bend 45° path between its endpoints when it is shorter and clear.
  Controlled by `OptimizationConfig.shorten_detours` (default on). Length-match
  and diff-pair gating is not added here (no worse than `pull_tight` today).
