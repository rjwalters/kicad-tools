- **`kct pcb reinforce` anchors each wire at its two ends by default**
  (Issue #8742). New `--anchor-mode ends|spaced` (default `ends`): the net's
  copper is split into single-layer legs and each leg gets exactly two
  anchors, one near each end and none mid-run, each `--end-offset` mm
  (default 1.0) clear, in straight-line distance, of the same-net copper it
  lands on (a pad or via its track overlaps, or a branch's whole trunk), using
  the pad's real shape and rotation. Both anchors are decided together: a
  leg never gets one anchor except when a clearance rule refused the other
  (`partial`, listed in `refused`). With
  `--current-paths`, every leg of every eligible path is anchored on each
  layer it uses. A Y junction is a trunk plus a branch; legs too short for two
  anchors are `too_short`, loops and higher-degree junctions `unhandled`, and
  unresolved declarations are listed in `unresolved_paths` with a stderr
  warning. `--format json` adds `anchor_mode`, `end_offset_mm`, `legs` and
  `unresolved_paths`; `ReinforceResult.legs` keeps each leg's polyline and
  anchors. The anchors are still tented vias with no mask opening until #8743.
