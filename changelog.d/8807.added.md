- **`kct readiness --quantity N` and `--inventory-covered C123,C456`** (Issue #8807). The
  `bom` gate's LCSC stock check can now be run for the real order size (default 5) and can
  exclude catalog ids held in your own fab parts inventory, with the same semantics as
  `kct export --boards` / `kct submit-jlcpcb --inventory-covered`. Both are recorded in the
  report and `README.txt` (covered ids are listed as "held in operator inventory, not
  stock-verified"; a covered id that is not short or not in the BOM is flagged). `--verify`
  re-evaluates the manifest's new per-part stock data at the requested values, and refuses
  (naming the regenerate command) on an older bundle that lacks it. `--generate` and
  `--verify` now share one verdict function, so a shortfall is worded identically; any
  pass/fail difference comes from the recorded stock observation (live vs unverified).
