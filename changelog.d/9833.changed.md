- **`kct sch` connectivity tools match `kicad-cli` on T-junctions, mid-wire pins and mid-wire power symbols** (Issue #9833). `build_wire_graph` connected three things the KiCad netlist leaves open: a wire ending on another wire's middle with no junction dot, a pin lying on a wire's middle, and a power symbol on a wire's middle. Wires now join at shared endpoints or at a junction dot only, labels still attach anywhere along a wire, and pins/power symbols attach at wire endpoints only — the rule `kicad-cli sch export netlist` implements (verified row by row against the pinned KiCad 10.0.6 image). `sch pin-map`, `sch connections` and `sch find-unconnected` share the graph, so open nets are no longer reported as connected. `sch connections` additionally warns about each undotted T / pin / power symbol on a wire's middle ("KiCad netlist treats this as open"), both in the table and as a `warnings` array in `--format json`, instead of silently treating it as connected. Net-tie bridging still works: the net-tie pins sit a fraction of a grid step off the wire ends, and the bridge now snaps to the nearest wire node.
