- **Coupled pair legs keep true-width clearance to foreign pads** (Issue #7597). The pair attach and the final leg re-verification now grow foreign pad keep-outs by the leg's width and class surcharge, and hold each foreign pad to its own router net-class clearance (`max(leg class, pad class)`; pair legs are never moved by the class-pad repair), so a wide pair class (e.g. 0.275 mm USB3) no longer ships a leg closer to another net's pad than its clearance allows.
