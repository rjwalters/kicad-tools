- **Stock schematic blocks build with their defaults and anchor ports on real
  pins and wires** (Part of Issue #7402). A real-symbol smoke test builds every
  exported `kicad_tools.schematic.blocks` factory against the KiCad 10 libraries.
  `DebugHeader(interface="jtag")`, `UCC27211GateDriver` and
  `USBConnector(esd_protection=True)` now build with default arguments, and the
  USB ESD array is wired in rather than left orphaned (#7508). The CAN ESD,
  `BackToBackFETPair`, `I2CPullups`, `create_gpio_pull_resistor` and
  `GateDriverBlock` ports now land on a pin end or wire, so connecting to them
  no longer silently fails (#7507, #7509). Ports at off-grid origins remain open.
