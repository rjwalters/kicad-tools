- **`kct optimize-traces` straightens a jog just before a pad** (Issue #8298). When a chain ends on a pad centre with a short sideways step just before it (a parallel run offset by the step), the run before the step is extended along its own line into the pad and the step is dropped. Pads only, never vias; only when the end stays within half the trace width of the pad centre, the new segment is collision-clear, and length does not grow. Part of the lateral-jog pass (`--no-jogs` disables both).
