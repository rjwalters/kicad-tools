- **`kct parts` no longer categorizes LEDs as inductors** (Issue #9574).
  LCSC descriptions containing "Indication" substring-matched the inductor
  abbreviation (`" ind"`); the inductor abbreviation now matches on a word
  boundary, and a description naming both a diode and an LED
  ("Discrete Diode LED Indication - Discrete") lands on `LED`. Standalone
  "LED" mentions match on a word boundary with a "Non-LED" negation guard,
  and parts that merely mention an LED (connectors with an integrated LED,
  LED-driver regulators) keep their own category.
