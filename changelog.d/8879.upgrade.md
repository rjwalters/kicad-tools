- **Re-key `silk_over_mask_opening` waivers on non-zero-origin boards** (Issue #8879). The mask-opening label (`mask opening (fp_*/gr_* …) at (x, y)`) is the second item of the violation and is matched verbatim by waivers, so its coordinates changing from board-relative to sheet-absolute means any waiver or keyed waiver that quotes the old coordinates stops matching. Re-run `kct check` and update those waivers with the new coordinates. Boards with origin (0, 0) are unaffected.
