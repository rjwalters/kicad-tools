- **`mask_to_copper` no longer reports a pad's own thermal-spoke pour as exposed
  trace** (Issue #9284). A plain pad joined to its own same-net pour by thermal
  spokes is now disposition `own_pour_thermal` at info instead of an error, when
  pcbnew declares thermal relief between the two, the fill shows it, nothing is
  exposed beyond the pad, and within the policy's examined band (0.15 mm for
  JLCPCB) the only copper under mask beside the opening is 1 to 4 spokes no wider
  than declared. JLCDFM listed no item for four such pads on board 01 (relief gaps
  0.2 and 0.3 mm, 0 mm expansion; #8858). Solid connections, same-net pour not
  connected to the pad, a pour body inside 0.15 mm of the opening, pour exposed
  through a positive mask expansion, and different-net pour keep their verdict.
  On boards 00-07 this applied to 94 errors (board 03: 45 of 51 own-pour
  findings; its 6 solid ones stay errors). Measurements carry three new optional
  fields, `pour_spokes`, `pour_relief_gap_mm` and `pour_spoke_width_mm`.
