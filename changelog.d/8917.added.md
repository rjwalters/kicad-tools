- **`kct pcb current-paths-audit` reports each resolved path's narrowest copper** (Issue #8917, follow-up to #8741). `PathResolution.min_width` is surfaced as `min_width_mm`, `min_width_at` (sheet coordinates of that segment's midpoint), `min_width_layer` and `min_width_parallel_group` in `--format json`, and as a `min width 0.200 mm on F.Cu at (x, y)` line in text output, so a 0.2 mm neck on a 30 A path is visible in the command people read to judge a path rather than only in `kct check`'s `path_ampacity` error. A deliberately doubled stretch counts once at the **summed** width `path_ampacity` already prints for that parallel group (flagged as such), Kelvin sense taps are excluded, and `unresolved`/`ambiguous` paths carry `null` rather than `0`.
