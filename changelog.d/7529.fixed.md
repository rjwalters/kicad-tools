- **JLC upload ledger no longer crashes on Windows** (Issue #7529). The
  `UploadLedger`, upload lock and the submission-plan read/inventory helpers
  guard the POSIX-only `O_NOFOLLOW` / `O_DIRECTORY` / `dir_fd` primitives,
  falling back to `islink` checks and skipping the directory fsync where they
  are missing.
