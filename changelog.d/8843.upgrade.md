- **`kct check --mfr jlcpcb` can newly fail** (Issue #8843). Boards with copper
  gaps under 0.10 mm (even same-net), pads under 0.20 mm apart on 2 oz copper, or
  through-hole component pads within 2.0 mm of an SMD pad now report errors. A
  board with no declared copper weight gets a `physical_copper_gap_incomplete`
  info finding (not an error). Pass an explicit `--physical-copper-gap MM` to override
  the gap threshold; other manufacturers are unchanged.
