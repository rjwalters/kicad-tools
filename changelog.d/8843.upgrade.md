- **`kct check --mfr jlcpcb` can newly fail** (Issue #8843). Boards with copper
  gaps under 0.10 mm (even same-net), pads under 0.20 mm apart on 2 oz copper, a
  2 oz board with no declared copper weight, or THT/vias within 2.0 mm of an SMD
  pad now report errors. Pass an explicit `--physical-copper-gap MM` to override
  the gap threshold; other manufacturers are unchanged.
