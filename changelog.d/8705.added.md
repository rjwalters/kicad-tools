- **`kct sync` reports copper stranded by a pad-net change** (Issue #8705). Tracks and vias still carrying a pad's old net and attached to that pad appear under `stranded_copper` in `--analyze` (exit 2). New opt-in `--remove-stranded` (with `--apply`, subject to `--dry-run`/`--confirm`) removes them; locked tracks are never removed.
