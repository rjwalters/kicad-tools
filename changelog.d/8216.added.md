- **`kct pcb prune` removes redundant copper** (Issue #8216). Builds per-net connectivity from copper geometry, removes redundant branches and same-layer loops only when every pad and pad-to-zone connection stays connected, snaps off-centre ends onto via/pad centres, and (with `kicad-cli`) drops any net's edits that add DRC violations under `pcb drc --refill-zones`.
