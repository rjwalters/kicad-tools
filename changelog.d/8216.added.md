- **`kct pcb prune` removes redundant copper** (Issue #8216). Builds per-net connectivity the way KiCad does (endpoint-anchored track joins, track ends inside the full pad/via shape — never capsule overlap), and is deliberately conservative: a removal must keep every live neighbour connected through track/via/pad joins (a zone outline is never relied on, since its fill can void), keep every pad/zone terminal connected, and never leave a track end joined only by overlap. Off-centre ends are snapped onto the via/pad centre together with every end that shares the joint. With `kicad-cli`, edits that add violations under `pcb drc --refill-zones` (run with the board's own `.kicad_pro`) are dropped; without a DRC check (`--no-drc` or no `kicad-cli`) the file is not written unless `--unverified` is passed. Intentionally kept for now: a branch whose copper touches other same-net copper without a shared endpoint (such as a redundant spur beside a via) is protected rather than removed, and collinear grid-step runs are not merged.
