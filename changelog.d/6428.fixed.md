- **Pour completion no longer trades one net's links for another's** (Issue #6428).
  `kct route`'s KiCad-oracle completion loop kept a round when the pour
  links summed over all nets fell, so a weld that closed 17 GND links by
  cutting the +3.3V pour (17 GND -> 1 +3.3V) counted as progress and the run
  ended `no_progress` with exit 3. A round may now not raise any net's link
  count: the copper near the new link is withdrawn and the round retried, or
  the board is restored. The stitcher's pad-to-via stub checks (straight,
  dog-leg and multi-leg) now test only foreign tracks on the stub's own
  layer, and the plane-via reservation's escape-corridor guard treats a
  foreign pad, keep-out or board edge beside a signal pin as a blocked side.
