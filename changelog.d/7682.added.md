- **3D model coverage audit counts footprints, not model refs** (Issue #7682). New `kct pcb model-audit` classifies every footprint (`resolved`, `embedded`, `dangling`, `no_ref`, `unresolved_var`, `hidden_only`, `lcsc_cache_miss`, `placeholder`, `exempt`) under a `host`, `pinned` or `--model-dir` environment, with a pin-count wrong-body heuristic. `kct check` gains a `model_coverage` category (warnings), `kct render` reports `partial` with a reason per bare footprint instead of `passed` with 0 models checked, `kct fleet status` gains a `3D` column, and a non-blocking CI job uploads `model-coverage.json`.
