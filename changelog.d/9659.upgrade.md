- **Exported creepage rules can newly tighten on boards with negative or
  range nets** (Issue #9659). `kct creepage export-rules` now assigns
  sign-aware domain netclasses (`kct_m150V` for a `-150 V` net,
  `kct_m150Vto150V` for a `[-150, +150]` range) instead of one magnitude
  class, so re-emitting on a board with bipolar or swinging nets produces
  **new and larger** clearance rules that a downstream `kicad-cli pcb drc`
  may newly flag. Re-run `kct creepage export-rules` (and re-run DRC) on such
  boards to pick up the corrected, tighter rules.
