- **`kct route-auto` refuses more routes** (Issues #6139, #6122). Copper
  within the board's hole clearance of a drilled hole (0.25 mm by default), or
  within its edge clearance of an NPTH slot (0.5 mm by default), is now
  refused and not written. So is copper closer to a net in a larger netclass,
  such as `HV`, than that class requires, and copper that breaks a
  `.kicad_dru` rule whose condition the gate cannot evaluate (such as
  `insideArea()`): that rule is applied at its larger value. Such a net now
  fails and exits 1 instead of producing a board that KiCad's DRC rejects.
  Under `--strategy auto` it is retried with `hierarchical`. Boards with an
  unconditional `.kicad_dru` clearance below their `Default` netclass are now
  gated at the rule's value, as KiCad's own DRC is.
