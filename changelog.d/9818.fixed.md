- **Saved coordinates keep 0.0001 mm precision past 100 mm** (Issue #9818).
  The S-expression writer formatted newly written floats with six
  significant digits, so `147.5625` was saved as `147.562`, which tilted
  exact 45-degree copper (e.g. from `PCB.add_trace`) off-angle in the fleet
  angle census. New floats of magnitude 1 or more are now written with up
  to six decimals (1 nm), trailing zeros trimmed. Smaller values keep their
  six significant digits. Parsed numeric tokens keep their original
  spelling, so files that are only loaded and saved come out byte-identical.
