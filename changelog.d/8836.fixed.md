- **`kct check --mfr jlcpcb` applies JLCPCB's 2 oz copper floors** (Issue #8836). The
  `4layer_2oz` profile now carries the 0.1524 mm outer-layer clearance floor (inner layers
  keep 0.1016 mm); the emitted `.kicad_dru` scopes them with `(layer outer)` / `(layer inner)`.
  `kct check` and `kct readiness` take the manufacturer preset from the board's declared
  stackup outer copper weight instead of defaulting to 1 oz, a stackup weight with no profile
  entry is an error rather than a silent 1 oz fallback, and the check header/JSON
  (`copper_weights.floors_outer_oz`) and readiness manifest (`copper_weights`) record the weights.
