- **`kct pcb reinforce --strategy parallel|auto` reinforces short links with
  parallel copper** (Issue #8916). `parallel` copies each selected leg, vertex
  for vertex and at the same width, onto the opposite outer layer, tied to it
  at both ends (the through-hole pad the leg ends on, else a stitching via) and
  by stitching vias every ~5 mm (the board's default via, never closer than
  `kct stitch`'s same-net spacing); no anchors, no mask opening. Every copy
  track and via passes the anchor clearance checks (class floors, `.kicad_dru`
  rules, `--voltage-map` creepage, hole-to-hole, hole-to-copper); a copy that
  does not fit whole is refused and reported with the obstacle net and rule,
  never written in part. `auto` copies a leg shorter than the minimum wire
  length (15 mm by default) and gives every longer leg its buttress wire.
  `wire` stays the default; `parallel`/`auto` need `--anchor-mode ends` and
  exit with an error under `spaced`. The run report and `--format json` name
  the strategy per leg, the wire-list sidecar records each copy (so `--clear` /
  `--replace` remove it), `reinforcement_incomplete` no longer asks a copied
  leg for a wire, and re-running adds no copper. `path_ampacity` judges the
  pair on its summed cross-section, and no longer reports a stitching via
  inside doubled copper as an under-capacity layer change.
