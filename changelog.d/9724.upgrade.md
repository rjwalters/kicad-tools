- **Copper-to-edge findings in the 100-500 nm band now pass `kct check`** (Issue #9724). Copper-to-edge verdicts for traces, SMD pads and zone copper that used to fail for shortfalls between 0.1 um and 0.5 um under the requirement now pass, matching pinned KiCad 10.0.6. If you relied on kct flagging that band (e.g. a fab whose edge-clearance rule is stricter than KiCad's), re-check your boards against the fab's own rules. Via and through-hole-pad verdicts against `min_hole_to_edge_mm` are unchanged and still flag any shortfall beyond 0.1 um.
