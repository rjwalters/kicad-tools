- **`kct submit-jlcpcb --dry-run` no longer refuses stock `kct export --mfr jlcpcb` bundles** (Issue #8538). The JLCPCB BOM `Footprint` and CPL `Package` columns now both use the bare footprint name, the preflight ignores a `Library:` prefix when comparing (so older bundles pass), and a genuine mismatch names the reference plus the BOM and CPL values.
