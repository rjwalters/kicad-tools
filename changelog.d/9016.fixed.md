- **`kct stitch --mfr` rejects same-net via-to-pad gaps in the warning band** (Issue #9016). The stitcher's pad-gap band edge now uses the detection edge (`GapBand.detection_edge_mm`) instead of the error edge, matching `kct check --mfr`; on jlcpcb `4layer_2oz` this means gaps in `[0.13, 0.15)` mm are no longer seated. Stackups without a separate warning band are unchanged.
