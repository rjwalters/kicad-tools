- **`kct check` flags small passives with both pads solid to pours** (Issue #9796). The `thermal_asymmetry` rule additionally reports a 2-pad passive of 0402 class or smaller (outer pad copper span ≤ 1.75 mm) whose both terminals are effectively solid to a same-net zone fill -- each pad acts as a heatsink, giving cold or incomplete joints and hard rework. Warning severity, same waiver keys as the existing thermal findings; threshold sources in `docs/research/thermal-relief-small-passives.md`.
