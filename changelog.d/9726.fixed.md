- **`kct check` no longer reports off-path copper for match groups it skips** (Issue #9726). A partially routed group — any unrouted member, an unrouted differential-pair leg, or fewer than two measured lanes — is omitted from the skew report and now emits no off-path copper warnings either; the warning appears only alongside a derived skew figure, for every single-ended member and both legs of each differential pair of the eligible group.
