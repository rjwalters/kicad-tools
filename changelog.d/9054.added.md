- **`kct fix-silkscreen --clip-to-holes` clips footprint silk off drilled holes** (Issue #9054). This is an explicit post-routing step (the router never calls it, #5744). It clips `fp_line`, `fp_poly` and `fp_rect` silk against every via, PTH and NPTH drill, removing only the silk needed to clear the hole. A crossed line keeps its clear ends. A filled polygon loses only the area around the drill; if the drill sits inside the shape, the polygon is written with a zero-width keyhole bridge. An unfilled outline is rewritten as lines. The clearance defaults to the `silk_hole_overlap` rule's own `SILK_HOLE_CLEARANCE_MM` (0 mm), and `--hole-clearance` overrides it. Arcs and circles are reported rather than clipped, and any graphic still over a hole fails the command. Text is left for `place-silk-refs --over-holes`. Library entry point: `SilkscreenRepairer.repair_hole_clearance()`.
