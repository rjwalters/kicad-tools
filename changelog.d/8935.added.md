- **`kct submit-jlcpcb --checkout`: opt-in, attended, exact-total-approved
  checkout of the saved PCB/PCBA cart** (Issue #8935). Off by default. After
  the website leg saves the cart, the operator, in their own terminal, can
  continue to JLC's payment step: `--auto` (type the final total back to approve
  it) or `--approve-total 'USD 123.45'`. The total is compared to the cent and
  currency after the payment method is chosen and again just before the single
  pay click; any mismatch stops without paying. Without `--pay` it is a
  rehearsal that stops before the pay button. The saved site payment method is
  the default; `--card-file` (mode 600, owned by you, outside every git
  repository) is optional and its values never reach output, logs, receipts or
  screenshots. "ordered" is recorded only from JLC's confirmation page, in an
  order ledger (`~/.kct/orders/jlc-orders.jsonl`) that stops a second order for
  the same plan; an unconfirmed payment exits 3 and is never retried. Refused
  without an interactive foreground terminal and under CI, schedulers, Loom,
  agent harnesses and MCP. Selectors are unverified against the live site, so
  every step asks the operator to confirm it.
