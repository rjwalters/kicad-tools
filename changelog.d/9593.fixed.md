- **Boards whose outline lives in a footprint now work** (Issue #9593).
  KiCad treats footprint-local `Edge.Cuts` graphics as board outline (e.g.
  SparkFun's IoT RedBoard ESP32-P4); every outline consumer - bounds,
  `kct check` edge clearance, router keep-in, placement, silkscreen, DSN
  export, boardview - now reads them, transformed by the footprint's
  position/rotation (no extra mirror for back-side footprints: KiCad
  stores their local geometry already mirrored, so the outline lands where
  `kicad-cli` puts it). `fp_poly`/`fp_circle`/`fp_curve` outlines reach
  the edge-clearance checker like `fp_rect` does, a circular cut-out no
  longer replaces the whole board in `BoardGeometry`, the placement
  optimizer keeps parts out of footprint cut-outs, an open `fp_curve` is
  no longer closed into a phantom contour, and legacy KiCad 5 `(module
  ...)` blocks contribute their `Edge.Cuts` too. Verified against
  `kicad-cli pcb drc` (pinned 10.0.6) on committed fixtures and on
  rect/poly/circle/curve and back-side variants.
