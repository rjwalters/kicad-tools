- **`kct check` verifies buttress-wire reinforcement is buildable** (Issue #8746, #8741 slice 4b). New `reinforcement_incomplete` category, active only with a `reinforcement_eligible` current path: per wire leg it checks two end anchors, a continuous solder-mask opening, mask-open anchor rings, no layer change through a via smaller than the anchor drill, and no stray anchors. Errors on nets whose ampacity waiver cites reinforcement (`reinforc|buttress|wire`), warnings otherwise.
