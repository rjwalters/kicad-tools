- **The router repairs the acute two-segment fold-backs `kct check`'s `sharp_trace_corner` gates** (Issue #8919). A track that overshoots and turns back 135 degrees leaves an open 45-degree wedge of etchant-trapping copper -- the shape JLC's DFM tool flagged on the #8840 calibration board. The trace optimizer could never reach these: `shorten_detour_pass` is a no-op below three segments, and a two-segment fold-back is exactly that case. `cleanup_artifacts` gains a Step 6 that moves the vertex along the longer leg into a 90- or 135-degree corner, or collapses the whole detour to the shortest 45-degree path between its far ends, reporting `acute_foldbacks_repaired` and `acute_foldbacks_skipped`. Detection matches the `sharp_trace_corner` rule exactly (angle constants imported from `kicad_tools.explain.checks.acid_trap`, inscribed-circle pad/via burial, the `s0` open-wedge test), so a vertex the gate exempts is left untouched; every candidate is clearance-checked, keeps the net's pad/via anchors on copper and must introduce no new acute corner, and a vetoed repair is counted rather than forced.
