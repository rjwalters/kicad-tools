- **`PCB.add_trace` / `PCB.add_via` accept a net NUMBER as the `net` argument** (Issue #9078). An int is now resolved through the board's net table instead of being treated as a net *name*: previously `add_trace(..., net=5)` silently minted a phantom net named `"5"` (`(net 13 5)`) and landed the new copper on it, shorting it against the real net 5 the pads and vias still carried — the defect behind board 04's five fresh-regen `SHORT: '5' and 'OSC_OUT'`-family findings. An int that names no net now raises `ValueError` instead of creating a net.
