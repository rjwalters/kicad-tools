- **Relief-rescue log lines now explain themselves** (Issue #8256). The "no relief path" line replaces `freed width unknown` with a lower bound on the corridor the ripped blockers vacated (widest ripped trace plus twice its resolved clearance) and an upper bound on the shortfall in mm. When no blocker was ripped yet, it says so instead of printing a shortfall. It also states whether copper placed since the rip re-occupied that corridor, naming the net. `Targeted rip-up resolved X/Y` now carries a per-net reason count when X < Y: rolled back, rip skipped, refused by the rip-up guard, no blockers found, or fewer than 2 pads. Diagnostics only; routing is unchanged.
