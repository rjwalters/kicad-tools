- **Report-only barrier-closure diagnostic for the negotiated router** (Issue #9000).
  Setting `KCT_BARRIER_CLOSURE=1` makes `route_all_negotiated` track a per-layer
  union-find over coarse board regions and log each committed route that closes a
  loop of copper, with the still-unrouted nets the loop cuts in two. Off by
  default; routing results are unchanged with it on or off.
