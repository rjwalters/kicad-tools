- **`optimize-traces` pad-escape guard now follows each segment's own layer on a
  multi-layer Kelvin branch** (Issue #9660). The guard resolved the guarded
  copper layer from the chain's first segment only, so on a Kelvin branch whose
  legs meet through a via (the `KelvinBoardGate.optimize_net` path) openings on
  any other layer were missed while openings that cannot expose the segment's
  layer could falsely veto its rewrite. The layer is now resolved per segment:
  new copper is checked against the openings of its own layer, the
  surviving-endpoint and already-carried-copper comparisons also require the
  same layer, and a mixed-layer branch is guarded on every layer it touches.
