- **`kct pcb reinforce` no longer plans wire runs under same-side courtyards** (Issue #8821). In `ends` mode a leg whose run would overlap a same-side component courtyard is refused (footprint named in the leg reason, also under `--dry-run`), and when picking the layer to process it prefers the side with no overlap. It reuses the geometry of the `wire_run_courtyard_overlap` check via a shared helper.
