- **`kct creepage export-rules` no longer collapses signed voltages onto one
  magnitude domain** (Issue #9659). Nets of opposite polarity (`+150 V` vs
  `-150 V`) or sign-flipping ranges (`[-150, +150]`) used to share a single
  `kct_150V` class, so no clearance rule was emitted between them and the
  exported KiCad rules held those pairs at the DRU floor while the census read
  them as 300 V apart. Domains are now keyed by signed interval and pairs are
  differenced end-to-end (`dv = max(|a.hi - b.lo|, |b.hi - a.lo|)`), so a
  +150/-150 pair gets its 3.2 mm rule (IEC 60664-1 basic, PD2/IIIa) and a
  swinging `[-150, +150]` domain gets a same-class rule against itself.
  All-positive scalar maps keep their exact previous netclass and rule names.
