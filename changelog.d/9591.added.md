- **`kct check` flags planes cut into pieces by another net's tracks** (Issue #9591). New
  `plane_split` category (warning, advisory, non-gating; `--skip plane_split` disables it)
  reports a zone whose outline splits into more pad-bearing pieces because of foreign
  tracks, and lists each piece with its pads and vias ("piece 2: C20.1, U1.62"). Judged
  geometrically on the zone outline, so unfilled zones are covered. Router-side zone-crossing
  cost (#9597) and the net-status piece listing (#9598) are tracked separately.
