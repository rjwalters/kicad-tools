- **Footprint Edge.Cuts arcs and rounded rects are board edge again** (Issue #9759).
  An `fp_poly` whose `(pts ...)` carries `(arc ...)` segments no longer loses
  those arcs from the stroke outline, and an `fp_rect` with `(radius N)` no
  longer reports square corners: `PCB.get_board_outline_segments`, board
  geometry, boardview, the router outline reader and board bounds now follow
  the authored arc and rounded corner, through the same reading the filled
  cut-out side uses (#9692). Such a footprint cut-out inside the board
  outline subtracts its true area from board geometry (router, placement,
  keep-out and panel inputs), and a stadium-shaped `fp_rect` whose radius is
  half its width keeps the board outline. `kct check`'s edge-clearance rule therefore
  flags copper just outside an arc bulge or rounded corner (and copper in an
  inward arc's notch) and stops flagging copper near a square corner the
  rect does not have, matching pinned kicad-cli 10.0.6 on every probe.
