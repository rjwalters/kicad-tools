- **Lattice engine routes pads sealed inside their own slit-band shell** (Issue #9708). Since the same-net pad slit band (#9060), a pad whose whole near escape cloud sits inside the fab `pad_spacing` band around its own outline declined `no-path`: every outgoing lattice edge of the cloud is band-interior judged per-edge, even though the same straight pad exit as one segment unions the pad and is manufacturing-legal (subdividing it into lattice edges changed the verdict), and the nearby vias were via-in-pad. Such a sealed pad now gains far escape landings that keep the band detection edge from every own-net SMD pad, so the router threads the straight exit out through the shell on connected copper the validator accepts. Pads that already escape keep their stub sets byte-for-byte, and genuine parallel-band slit legs stay rejected.
