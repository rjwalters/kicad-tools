- **Lattice pad-class repair no longer moves diff-pair copper** (Issue #6437).
  The #6395 post-negotiation repair now freezes every net of a detected diff
  pair (extra-pad stubs, uncoupled fallback legs and declined pairs routed
  single-ended), so it can no longer change a pair's skew; such shortfalls are
  reported as "on diff-pair nets left in place". Via-to-pad class shortfalls
  are now reported (not moved), and a `--voltage-map` board logs that the
  repair was skipped instead of skipping silently.
