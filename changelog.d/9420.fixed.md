- **`kct check` no longer reports a stock footprint's own same-net pads as errors** (Issue #9420). `physical_copper_gap` (native and fab-view) and `mask_to_copper` treated the ground-pad array of `RF_Module:ESP32-C3-WROOM-02` as routed copper: 0.19 mm between two of its pads, and a circular pad's opening exposing the rectangular pad under it. Same-net pads of **one** footprint now report at `info` (`mask_to_copper` disposition `within_own_footprint`); pads of different footprints, pads on different nets, and pad-to-track/via/zone gaps keep their verdict.
