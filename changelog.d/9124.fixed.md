- **Same-net pad slit-band guard now rejects tangent via seats** (Issue #9124).
  A via whose annulus is exactly tangent to its own SMD pad (centre one radius
  from a pad face or corner) touches it at a single point and leaves a sub-edge
  wedge the fab-view `physical_copper_gap` check flags; `same_net_pad_gap_acceptable`,
  `same_net_pad_gap_band_ring` and the C++ mirror (`BUILD_VERSION` 68, run
  `kct build-native`) no longer accept it as a copper union. Real overlap and
  the segment form are unchanged. Clears board 06's PCIE_RX+ finding.
