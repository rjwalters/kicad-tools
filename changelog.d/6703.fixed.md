- **Lattice uncoupled diff-pair fallback legs now route partner-aware**
  (Issue #6703). When a pair's coupled search declines, its two single-ended
  legs no longer route as unrelated nets: each leg order is tried, the second
  leg is pulled toward the first leg's corridor, legs are searched at the
  strictest pad class (so no class-pad shortfall gets a pair net demoted), and
  the best-matched leg pair is kept only when the remaining fallback pairs
  still route as well behind it. Board 07rf TMDS_D0/D1 raw skew drops from
  7.213/8.393 mm to 0.642/1.824 mm, all three TMDS pairs tune to within the
  0.075 mm `kct check` limit with one meander each, and their uncoupled length
  falls by 10-14 mm per pair. Boards with fallback pairs route slower (07rf:
  about 3x).
