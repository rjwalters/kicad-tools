- **`mask_to_copper` no longer errors on an on-pad track whose end cap pokes past the pad edge** (Issue #9261). A same-net straight segment with at least one authored end strictly inside its pad now gets the one-entry-crossing allowance (`1.45 × width × clearance`) even when its other end is also inside or sits on the edge, so a cap 0.02-0.05 mm past the edge reads `info` like a full exit instead of `error`. Pass-through, along-edge and straddle segments (no end inside) keep their error; arcs, vias and zones are unchanged.
