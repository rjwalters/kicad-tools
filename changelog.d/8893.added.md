- **`kct bom --check-distributor-stock` flags BOM lines with no DigiKey/Mouser stock** (Issue #8893). Needed quantity is `--quantity` × refs sharing an MPN, matched case-insensitively (the distributor APIs report their own casing). Exits 2 when any line is flagged, 0 with a notice when no distributor credentials are set. An MPN whose every lookup failed — HTTP error, exhausted quota, missing credentials — is reported as *not checked* rather than out of stock, and never flagged. Supports `--format json` and `--no-cache`.
