- **JLC checkout ledger hardening** (Issue #9151). Follow-ups from the
  PR #9101 review, all fail-closed and none touching payment logic: the
  ledger directory is now checked like the files (symlink, ownership,
  group/other-write refusals); the ledger refuses to open at all on a
  platform without `os.O_NOFOLLOW`; opens carry `O_NONBLOCK` so a
  swapped-in FIFO cannot hang the append; `locked()` wraps `mkdir`
  failures as `LedgerError`; `parse_page_money` refuses `CR`/`DR` glued
  to the amount and the words `CREDIT`/`DEBIT`; and the checkout outcome
  prints before browser teardown and review-page rendering, so a late
  failure can no longer hide the `ORDERED:` line.
