- **`EdgeClearanceRule` no longer computes the violation witness for every trace** (Issue #9715). `_check_segments` now resolves the outline-distance witness only when a finding is actually raised, restoring the pre-#9693 cost of `EdgeClearanceRule.check()` (the hoisted per-segment `_min_distance_to_outline` calls added +42–90% on clean boards, ~4.6 s on a tessellated-ring outline). Findings are unchanged.
