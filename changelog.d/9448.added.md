- **Projects can declare a smaller reference-designator height** (Issue #9448). New optional
  `requirements.manufacturing.silkscreen_min_reference_height_mm` in `project.kct` (>= 0.8 mm)
  relaxes the `silkscreen_text_height` floor for reference designators only: `kct check` and the
  generated `.kicad_dru` (a separate `Silkscreen Reference Height` rule; the label rule excludes
  designators via `!(A.Text == A.Reference)`) agree, and `kct readiness` lists the declaration as an
  accepted deviation. Labels and board text keep the fab floor (JLCPCB 1.0 mm, from its
  published capabilities). With no declaration, behaviour and the emitted DRU are unchanged.
