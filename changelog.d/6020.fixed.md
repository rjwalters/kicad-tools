- **`kct optimize-placement` models rotated and flipped parts the way it
  writes them** (Issue #6020). The pad transform used the y-up rotation, so
  every 90/270-degree footprint's pads were mirrored relative to the board
  KiCad opens; the overlap, boundary and clearance terms ignored rotation, so
  a turned part was checked as if it lay flat (the opposite of the
  slide-off pass, which could then turn a feasible optimum infeasible); and
  the optimizer could pick a board side the writer never applies. Pads now
  follow KiCad's orientation, bounding boxes swap at 90/270 degrees, side
  flags stay as on the board, and `--seed current` never returns a placement
  that scores worse than the one it started from (`--max-iterations 0` keeps
  it outright). Pad-anchored wirelength on rotated parts changes accordingly.
