- **`kct pcb move-footprint --map` takes a `side` per reference** (Issue #9471).
  An entry may carry `"side": "front" | "back"`; a part not already on that side
  is flipped (the `pcb-modify flip` transform) before `x`/`y`/`rotation` are
  applied, in one load/save. `rotation` is the stored KiCad angle written after
  the flip; documented with a two-pad THT example in `docs/reference/cli.md`.
