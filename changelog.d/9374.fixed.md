- **`mask_to_copper` net signatures keep every owner net of a merged mask
  region** (Issue #9374). A merged opening owned by pads on two or more nets
  previously named only the conductor net, so a different-net exposure matched a
  same-net identity-allowance entry. Measurements gain an optional `owner_nets`
  field and the finding's `nets` is built from it; single-owner and same-net
  signatures are unchanged.
