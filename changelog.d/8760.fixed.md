- **`kct route` no longer straightens tracks through keepout rule areas** (Issue #8760). The post-route optimizer treated `(tracks not_allowed)` areas as free space, so a detour could be collapsed into a straight track that kicad-cli reported as `items_not_allowed`; it now refuses such shortcuts, and `kct route` warns (net and area named) if any committed track or via still lies inside a track/via-blocking rule area.
