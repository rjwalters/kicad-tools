- **`kct check` can newly fail on boards with custom pads** (Issue #9498).
  Copper in a custom pad's primitives (the SOT-89 tab, for one) is now
  measured by `clearance`, silk-over-copper, `tht_smd_spacing` and
  `physical_copper_gap`, and a custom pad the outline builder refuses
  surfaces as a named `clearance_incomplete` /
  `silk_over_copper_incomplete` / `tht_smd_spacing_incomplete` (info) or
  `physical_copper_gap_incomplete` (error, that module's existing channel)
  finding. A board that previously passed while a different-net track ran
  through or near a custom pad's tab copper will now fail; move the copper
  or waive the pair.
