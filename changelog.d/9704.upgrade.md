- **`kct creepage` and `kct audit` can newly fail boards with cross-face bore
  paths** (Issue #9704). Boards where an HV conductor and another net approach
  through a non-plated hole, a milled slot or around the board edge were
  silently given an unmeasured (optimistic) creepage; those paths are now in
  the census and gate. Re-run `kct creepage` on HV boards that pass today --
  a new `via_bore` row marks the newly measured path, and the report's
  `board_thickness_mm` / `board_thickness_source` record the thickness
  assumption (stackup sum, or the 1.6 mm default when the board file declares
  none -- set a stackup to override it).
