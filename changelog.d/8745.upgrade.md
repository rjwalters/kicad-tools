- **`path_ampacity` can newly fail on layer-changing paths** (Issue #8745). A declared current path that crosses layers through vias whose combined capacity is below its declared current (e.g. 15 A through one 0.3 mm via) now reports an error (warning if `reinforcement_eligible` is false). Add vias / a via array, re-route on one layer, or waive the `path_ampacity` finding with a reason.
