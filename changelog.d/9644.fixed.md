- **`kct route-link` honors `_pair_bounds` caps when a paired net is missing from the voltage map** (Issue #9644). The omitted-net fallback now bounds the pair's ΔV by an authored device cap, matching `kct creepage` (e.g. a 300 V net capped at 40 V routes to a 1.100 mm floor, not 3.200 mm); a cap below `--hv-threshold` keeps the pair's floor at the capped lookup (25 V → 0.500 mm), as the census still scores the pair there, and an uncapped pair keeps the full spacing. The "absent from the voltage map" typo warning is unchanged.
