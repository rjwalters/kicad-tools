- **`silk_over_mask_opening` messages quote sheet-absolute coordinates** (Issue #8879). The `mask opening (fp_*/gr_* …) at (x, y)` text used board-relative coordinates, so on a board with a non-zero origin it disagreed with the PCB file's `(at ...)` values. The mask-opening label is part of the violation's items and therefore takes part in waiver matching, so on non-zero-origin boards waivers that quote the old coordinates must be re-keyed (see the upgrade note).
