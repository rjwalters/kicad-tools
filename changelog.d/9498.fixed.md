- **`kct check` sees custom-pad primitive copper** (Issue #9498). The
  `clearance`, silk-over-copper, `tht_smd_spacing` and `physical_copper_gap`
  rules now measure a custom pad by its anchor plus filled primitives (the
  same outline builder connectivity and the router already honour), so a
  short or a clearance violation against SOT-89-style tab copper is reported
  instead of reading as clean. A custom pad whose shape that builder refuses
  (`gr_circle`, stroked `gr_poly`, ...) gets a named incomplete finding (pad
  and primitive kind) from each of those rules instead of a silent
  anchor-only result; implementing the refused primitive kinds is tracked in
  #9501.
