- **`thermal_asymmetry` can newly fire on boards that were clean** (Issue #9796). A small (≤0402-class, pad copper span ≤ 1.75 mm) 2-pad passive with both pads solid to same-net pours now warns (case 1). Fix such findings with `kct zones relieve` or a pad/footprint `zone_connect` override, not a waiver. Footprint-level `(zone_connect N)` tokens are now honoured for every `thermal_asymmetry` finding, so a board that silently relied on them being ignored may see corrected modes.
