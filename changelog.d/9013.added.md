- **`kct route --detour-repair` runs a targeted post-settle detour repair
  pass** (Issue #9013). After the negotiated/escape loop settles, connections
  routed above 1.25x their alone-on-empty cost (#9003 probe) get one bounded
  repair attempt each, worst first: the few nets blocking the offender's
  short corridor are lifted, the offender is re-routed the short way, the
  lifted nets re-land, and the rearrangement is kept only if the whole-board
  score is strictly better AND the involved nets' copper shrank (the score
  alone can trade length for overflow) — otherwise the attempt is rolled
  back exactly. Off by default; without the flag routed output is
  byte-identical. The #9003 `--report-detour-ratio` report stays opt-in
  separately.
