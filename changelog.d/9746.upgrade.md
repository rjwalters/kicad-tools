- **`kct creepage` and `kct audit` can newly fail thin boards with cross-face
  paths** (Issue #9746). A board thinner than 1.6 mm that declares its
  thickness only in `(general (thickness X))` (no stackup) was measured as if
  it were 1.6 mm thick, overstating every cross-face bore/slot/edge path by
  the difference. Re-run `kct creepage` on such HV boards; a `via_bore` row
  whose `board_thickness_source` is now `"general"` reflects the real, shorter
  path.
