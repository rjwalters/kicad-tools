- **Opt-in net-turning pricing for the coupled lattice pair search** (Issue
  #7721). With `KCT_LATTICE_PAIR_TURN_PRICING=1` the fat diff-pair A* carries
  heading and a bounded signed winding in its state and charges
  `pitch x |winding|` at the goal, so same-direction U-turn pairs cost more than
  balanced routes. Experimental and off by default: it cuts skew on most
  board 06/07 pairs but still raises it on a few, so default routing is unchanged.
