- **`kct pcb prune` reports sheet-absolute coordinates** (Issue #8954). On a board whose `board_origin` is not `(0, 0)`, every coordinate in prune's report was board-relative and could not be found in the KiCad GUI. `stubs_added[].start`/`.end` (in `nets`, `rejected_by_drc` and `region_skipped`), the `stub {layer} {start} -> {end}` text line, `uncut_loops[].witness` (`start`/`end`/`position`, including `blocking_branches`) and the `uncut_loops[].segments` text now quote sheet-absolute positions, matching `pcb reinforce` and `pcb current-paths-audit` (#8493, #8798, #8928). Scripts that parse prune's JSON coordinates on a non-zero-origin board will see the shifted values; on a zero-origin board nothing changes. `uncut_loops` entries from the declared-net pass gain a structured `segment_ends` key (the endpoints behind the segment text). Library-level values (`PruneResult.uncut_loops`, `NetPlan.stubs`) stay board-relative.
