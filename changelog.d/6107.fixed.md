- **`kct route-auto` no longer writes shorts or clearance violations against
  other nets' pads, arcs and tracks** (Issue #6107). The `hierarchical`
  strategy now routes on the board as `kct route --nets` loads it, with every
  other net's pads, tracks, vias and arcs on its grid (including the C++
  grid), so it routes around them. The output gate from #6001 now also checks
  other nets' pads (real pad shapes) and arcs (true arc geometry, not the
  chord), and it enforces the board's copper clearance instead of overlap
  only. Refused copper is named, e.g. `shorts net(s) '/A' ... (e.g. pad R5.1
  at (115.000, 108.000) ...)`. Corridor strategies now warn once that they
  cannot route around other nets' copper.
