- **`kct check` no longer recommends staircase shortcuts that open a copper slit** (Issue
  #9862). `redundant_copper_staircase` cleared its candidates only against netclass
  clearance, mask openings and terminal contact, so a shortcut whose replacement copper
  hugs a same-net branch of another path (board 03 `RESET`, `BTN4`: 0.0828 mm vs the
  0.1016 mm floor) or a filled zone passed every gate and produced a new
  `physical_copper_gap` error once applied. Each candidate is now judged, complete at its
  emitted width, against the copper that remains after the original chain is removed --
  same-net branches and zone fills included -- under the active physical-gap policy (an
  explicit `--physical-copper-gap`, else the fab-view bands, where zone-fill gaps stay
  informational); a rejected bend falls through to the next candidate, so the same
  endpoints can still get a legal alternative.
