- **Schematic block ports follow the grid at off-grid origins** (Issue #7510). `CircuitBlock.ports` now snaps positions to the schematic's own grid (`Schematic(grid=...)`) whenever the schematic snaps symbols and wires (`SnapMode.AUTO` or `STRICT`), so ports land on their wire ends instead of missing them by the snap distance. `snap_to_grid` is now idempotent on grids whose points need more than 2 decimals (e.g. 0.635 mm), so an already-placed pin no longer re-snaps 0.01 mm off the wire drawn to it. On-grid layouts on the default 1.27 mm grid are unchanged apart from ports that previously missed their wire/pin by up to 0.8 mm.
