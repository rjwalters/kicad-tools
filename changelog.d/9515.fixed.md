- **`kct check` silk text boxes now cover KiCad's plotted ink** (Issue
  #9515). The stroked-text bounding box replaced its single
  `0.7 × size × len` width average with the measured KiCad stroke-font
  advance table (kicad-cli 10.0.6 gerber scan; advances quantize to
  size/21), plus a capped end-trim so the box tracks the last glyph's ink
  rather than its advance. The old model under-covered plotted ink by up to
  ~0.8 mm on a 4-character label and ~4 mm on a 10-character wide-glyph
  run, all of it at the far end of a justified label since #9433, so
  `silk_over_copper` / `silk_overlap` / `silk_hole_overlap` could miss a pad
  sitting inside KiCad's ink.
