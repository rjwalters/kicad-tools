- **`kct optimize-traces` no longer lays copper back over its own net** (Issue #8603).
  Staircase compression treated a serpentine rung as a staircase and always took the
  diagonal-first shortcut, whose closing leg ran back along the next segment or the
  net's other chain; on board 07's reviewed copper that raised
  `redundant_copper_covered` from 30 to 46. A shortcut is now tried diagonal-first, then
  orthogonal-first, and dropped if it lies on the net's own copper. Every pass's rewrite
  is checked the same way. The same run now leaves 15 covered findings, all of them
  already in the input.
