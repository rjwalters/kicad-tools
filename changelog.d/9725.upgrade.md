- **Via-group bars are judged per segment, and reinforce may anchor
  ambiguous nets** (Issue #9725). The bars of a recognised via group are
  checked at the full declared current one segment at a time. They are no
  longer treated as a two-layer parallel group, so a bar narrower than the
  path's required width can now fail `path_ampacity`. `kct pcb reinforce`
  now places anchors on the loop-free lane of a declared path that is
  `ambiguous` because of a loop; earlier releases placed none on that path.
  Run it with `--dry-run` to see the new anchors. The warning and JSON
  `unresolved_paths[].anchored_segments` give the count.
