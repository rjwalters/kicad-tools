- **`kct check` edge clearance can newly fail near footprint arcs and rounded rects** (Issue #9759).
  Copper within the edge clearance of an `(arc ...)` segment inside a
  footprint `fp_poly`, or of a rounded `fp_rect (radius N)` corner, on
  Edge.Cuts is now reported (kicad-cli already reported it). Re-run
  `kct check` on boards with such footprint outlines and move the copper;
  some findings next to the square corner of a rounded rect go away.
