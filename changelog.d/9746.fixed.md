- **`kct creepage` uses the board file's `(general (thickness X))` for
  cross-face paths** (Issue #9746). With no stackup declared, the bore, slot
  and edge-wrap paths always assumed a 1.6 mm board, so on a 0.8 mm board a
  path through a mounting hole was reported 0.8 mm longer than it is (3.75 mm
  instead of 2.95 mm) -- the optimistic direction. The thickness now comes
  from the stackup strata sum, else the board header's `general` thickness,
  else the 1.6 mm default; `board_thickness_source` reports `"general"` for
  the new middle case. The header value is exposed as `PCB.general_thickness`.
