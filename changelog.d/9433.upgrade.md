- **Silk findings may move for justified and back-side text** (Issue #9433). Silk text boxes now follow `(justify ...)` and mirroring, so a justified or `B.SilkS` label whose real ink overlaps copper or other silk that the old centred box missed can raise a new silk warning, and phantom ones disappear. `kct check --strict` exits 2 on warnings, so a board may newly fail it.
