- **`kct`'s managed `.kicad_dru` fab-floor block no longer lowers zone and SMD pad clearances** (Issue #8937). The unconditional `Clearance` custom rule *replaces* (KiCad custom rules are not floors) a zone's own `connect_pads (clearance …)` during native refill, so every `kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones` filled pours at the fab minimum — measured as a 0.1021 mm pour-to-foreign-pad gap where the design authorised 0.3 mm. Each zone's authored clearance is now restored by a zone-scoped `Zone clearance - <net> (<layer>)` rule at `max(fab floor, authored)`, emitted after the floor in one ascending-clearance sequence with the `Reviewed clearance` net-class rules (measured on the pinned kicad-cli 10.0.6: the zone-scoped condition is honoured by `--refill-zones` itself, refilling the fixture at 0.3005 mm; a zone authored below the floor still refills at the floor). The conditional `SMD Pad Clearance` rule gets the same `max()` treatment against the weakest protected net class. `KCT_PRESERVE_BOARD_RULES=0` still emits the plain profile block, by design.
