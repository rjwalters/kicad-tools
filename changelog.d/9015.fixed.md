- **`kct` no longer drops net-class clearance protection on inner layers of 2 oz boards** (Issue #9015). The `Reviewed clearance - <class>` rule's redundancy check compared the class's authored clearance against the outer-layer floor only, so on the split-floor profiles (Issue #8836: outer 0.15 mm, inner 0.1016 mm on `jlcpcb` 4-layer 2 oz) a class authored between the two floors — e.g. 0.12 mm — was skipped entirely, leaving its inner-layer pairs bound only at the 0.1016 mm inner floor. The skip predicate is now per layer (reusing the #8937 zone arm's `_zone_clearance_floor` helper): a class between the floors gets its rule emitted scoped `(layer inner)` at the authored value, while the outer floor keeps binding outer-layer pairs; classes at or below the inner floor still emit nothing, and unsplit (single-floor) profiles emit byte-identical `.kicad_dru` output.
