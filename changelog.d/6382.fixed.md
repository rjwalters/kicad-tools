- **Grid router keeps fine-pitch pins' straight-out lanes clear** (Issue #6382).
  Every long-axis pad of a detected fine-pitch package now carries a soft
  "entry lane" cost (one pin pitch wide, 2 mm past the copper tip, all layers)
  that other nets pay to cross, so wide escapes turn further out instead of
  sealing their neighbours' pocket. The cost also applies on the default
  `--grid auto` path. On board 05 (grid, 4 layers) EN_B and nFAULT now route;
  GND_SENSE and VM's U2.11 pocket remain open. `KCT_DISABLE_PAD_LANES=1` turns
  it off; `KCT_PAD_LANE_LENGTH_MM` / `KCT_PAD_LANE_PENALTY` tune it.

- **Grid router can charge a foreign via's full disc against fine-pitch pad lanes (opt-in)** (Issue #6382). The C++ pad-channel cost charges a via landing only at its centre cell, so a wide net's via can sit just past a neighbour pad's entry lane while its disc still seals it (board 05 PHASE_C vs U2.11). `PadChannelBudget` gains `charge_via_disc`, and `KCT_PAD_LANE_VIA_DISC=1` sets it on lane budgets. It is **off by default**: unset or `0` keeps centre-cell via charging, so routing is unchanged until board evidence supports flipping the default. `ROUTER_CPP_BUILD_VERSION` is 53 (rebuild with `kct build-native`). In the one board 05 run with it enabled, GND_SENSE still did not connect.
