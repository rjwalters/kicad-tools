- **Grid router keeps fine-pitch pins' straight-out lanes clear** (Issue #6382).
  Every long-axis pad of a detected fine-pitch package now carries a soft
  "entry lane" cost (one pad short side wide, 2 mm past the copper tip, all
  layers) that other nets pay to cross, so a wide net's 45-degree escape turns
  further out instead of sealing its neighbours' pocket. The cost also applies on
  the default `--grid auto` path. `KCT_DISABLE_PAD_LANES=1` turns it off;
  `KCT_PAD_LANE_LENGTH_MM` / `KCT_PAD_LANE_PENALTY` tune it.
