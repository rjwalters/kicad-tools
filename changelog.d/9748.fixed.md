- **`kct optimize-traces --drc-aware --mfr jlcpcb` no longer breaks mask-clean pad escapes** (Issue #9748). The pad-escape guard now vetoes two rewrite classes it missed: a last leg re-angled so it enters a pad's mask opening off-axis (a shallow crossing leaves copper beside the pad that the one-clean-entry allowance does not cover), and same-net copper slid into the mask-to-copper clearance band around another pad's opening (the JLCPCB policy floor, 0.09 mm). The `--drc-aware` referee also counts `mask_to_copper` now, over the pure-Python mask snapshot with the same policy `kct check --mfr` engages: growth in that category forces the per-net rollback even at a flat total, and the `DRC errors:` summary line reports `incl. mask_to_copper screen` when the count is included or `mask_to_copper not evaluated` when the snapshot cannot model the board (custom/chamfered pads, mask graphics, project `.kicad_dru` rules) — it never again claims bare `no regressions` while the mask rule went unseen.
