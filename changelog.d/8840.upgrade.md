- **New `kct check` warnings: `sharp_trace_corner`, `missing_plated_hole`** (Issue #8840).
  Boards that previously reported no warnings may now show these, and either can newly
  fail `kct check --strict`. `sharp_trace_corner` is skipped when the vertex is buried
  under same-net pad/via copper or the trace widths fill the wedge; skip it with
  `--skip sharp_trace_corner` until the copper is reworked. (Whether it should instead be
  non-gating like `pour_slot` is an open operator decision, not settled by this change.)
