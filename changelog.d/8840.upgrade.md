- **New `kct check` warnings: `sharp_trace_corner`, `missing_plated_hole`** (Issue #8840).
  Boards that previously reported no warnings may now show these, and either can newly
  fail `kct check --strict` and the boards' strict known-warnings gates; both are
  gating, not advisory. `sharp_trace_corner` is skipped when the vertex lies under
  same-net pad or via copper (through-vias count on every copper layer), when the
  trace widths fill the wedge, or when a thin trace lies inside a wider one; each
  vertex is reported once. To clear a finding, reroute the corner (or remove the
  acute branch) rather than skipping the rule.
