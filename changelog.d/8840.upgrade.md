- **New `kct check` warnings: `sharp_trace_corner`, `missing_plated_hole`** (Issue #8840).
  Boards that previously reported no warnings may now show these. `sharp_trace_corner`
  is advisory (non-gating, like `pour_slot`) and never fails `--strict` or the
  known-warnings gate; `missing_plated_hole` is a normal warning and can newly fail a
  strict gate on a board with co-located top/bottom SMD pads and no hole.
