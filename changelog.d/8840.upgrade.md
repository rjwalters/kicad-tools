- **New `kct check` warnings: `sharp_trace_corner`, `missing_plated_hole`** (Issue #8840).
  Both are on by default at warning severity. A plain `kct check` still exits 0 on them,
  but either can newly fail `kct check --strict` (exit 2) and the boards' strict
  known-warnings gates; both are gating, not advisory, and neither can be accepted in a
  board's `known_issues`. To clear a finding, fix the copper (reroute the corner, or join
  the pads with a plated hole), or record an evidence-bound waiver with
  `kct check --waive <key> --waive-reason ... --waive-reviewer ...`. `sharp_trace_corner`
  is skipped when the vertex lies inside same-net pad copper (the pad's real outline) or
  under a same-net via (through-vias count on every copper layer), when the trace widths
  fill the wedge, or when a thin trace lies inside a wider one; each vertex is reported
  once. It still reports junctions of three or more segments with an open wedge, which
  JLCDFM was not observed to flag (Issue #9037 tracks that over-reporting).
  `missing_plated_hole` reports co-located top/bottom fiducial pairs.
