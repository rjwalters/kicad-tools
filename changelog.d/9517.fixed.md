- **Lattice router no longer deadlocks pads whose stub ends inside the same-net slit band** (Issue #9517). The #9018 band guard judged every A* leg in isolation, so when a pad stub's end node sat within the fab `pad_spacing` band of its own pad, every leg leaving it was rejected and the connection declined `no-path` — board 05's VCP net (`U2.3`–`C5.1`) in the chained completion pass, the red nightly named in #9424. A band-interior leg that stays on the line of the pad-attached stub it leaves — the `check_physical_copper_gap` connector-coverage shape, not a slit — is now accepted; legs parallel to the pad edge inside the band (board 07's 14 slits) are still rejected.
