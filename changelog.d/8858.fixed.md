- **`mask_to_copper` no longer reports a pad's own land as exposed trace** (Issue #8858).
  A same-net track, pour, via or stacked pad that joins a pad lies partly
  under it. When the opening exposes no copper beyond the pad's own copper, the
  measurement is now `within_own_pad` at info instead of an error. The fab did
  not flag those entries, and there is no exposed trace to flag. This applied to
  1,292 of 1,542 errors on boards 00-07 (all 0 mm mask expansion), and to every
  one of them the pinned KiCad 10.0.6 plot showed 0 mm² of copper beyond the pad.
  Same-net copper *beside* a pad, a positive expansion's entry corridor,
  via-in-pad and different-net copper keep their verdict. Measurements carry a
  new optional `exposed_beyond_owner_mm2`.
