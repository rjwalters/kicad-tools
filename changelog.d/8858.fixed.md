- **`mask_to_copper` no longer reports a clean pad entry as exposed trace** (Issue #8858).
  A straight same-net track entering its own pad once, at 45° or steeper, is now
  disposition `within_own_pad` at info instead of an error, provided it exposes
  no copper beyond the pad and puts no more copper under mask beside the opening
  than that one crossing. A track lying wholly on its pad qualifies the same way.
  JLCDFM did not flag such entries. On boards 00-07 (all 0 mm mask expansion)
  this applied to 963 of 1,542 errors. Everything else keeps its verdict:
  tracks that run along, straddle or pass through the pad (the shapes the fab
  reported as danger), shallower entries, arcs, vias and pours touching the pad,
  entries through any expansion ring wider than about 10 µm, solder-mask-defined,
  custom and padstack pads, pads with no net, and different-net copper.
  Measurements carry two new optional fields, `exposed_beyond_owner_mm2` and
  `beside_owner_excess_mm2`.
