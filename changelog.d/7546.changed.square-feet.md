- **Skew bumps chamfer only their crowns, so the 0.2 mm #7542 pair at skew
  0.5 mm now tunes under its 0.8 mm uncoupled limit** (Issue #7546). Pinned
  kicad-cli 10.0.6 lets a bump's flat top couple to the partner once the
  corner where a leg meets the top has a 45-degree chamfer of at least half a
  trace width. A chamfer where the leg leaves the host line only leaves the
  partner copper beside it uncoupled. When its four-corner chamfer ladder
  can't close a pair, `tune_diff_pair_skew` now reruns the cascade with
  0.55-width crown chamfers on square feet, where each loop costs the partner
  two chamfers instead of four. It keeps that result if it tunes the pair, or
  leaves it no worse on skew and on the uncoupled figure. That pair reads
  0.72 mm in both engines; four chamfered corners read 0.94 mm and the square
  loop 0.90 mm. Pairs the ladder already tunes are unchanged. New
  `SerpentineConfig.foot_chamfer_widths` option (default `None`: same as
  `corner_chamfer_widths`).
