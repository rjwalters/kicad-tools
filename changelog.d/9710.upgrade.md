- **`kct optimize-placement` can newly report a creepage-infeasible placement**
  (Issue #9710). Boards whose `--voltage-map` has negative or range-valued nets,
  or whose `--hv-domains` declares negative voltages, now get the full signed
  pair spacing, so a placement accepted before can fail the creepage keepout.
  Re-run `kct optimize-placement` on those boards and re-check with
  `kct creepage`. Maps with only non-negative scalar voltages are unaffected.
