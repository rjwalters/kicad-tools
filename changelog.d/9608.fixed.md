- **`route_understated_pairs` honours `_pair_bounds` on `/`-prefixed net names**
  (Issue #9608). The detector keyed its bounds lookup with the voltage map's
  as-authored interval names while `_pair_bounds` entries are normalised (one
  leading `/` stripped), so on a KiCad-style map the cap was silently dropped and
  a pair explicitly declared at the same potential was falsely reported as
  understated. Interval keys are normalised before pairing, and returned
  `frozenset` keys are normalised either way, matching the docstring. The
  `kct voltage-map-merge` CLI path (already-normalised input) is unchanged.
