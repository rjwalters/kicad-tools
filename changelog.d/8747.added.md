- **`kct pcb reinforce --clear` / `--replace` remove or migrate old anchors** (Issue #8747). `--clear` removes the net's anchor vias, their solder-mask openings (#8743) and their wire records in the `.reinforcement.json` sidecar (#8744), and places none; `--replace` clears then places per `--anchor-mode` in one write. Anchors are identified from the sidecar when it lists them for the net, else by `--wire-gauge` anchor size. A removal that would leave the net without its only layer link is refused and reported (its opening stays); `--dry-run` lists the anchors and openings that would go. Refill zone pours afterward.
