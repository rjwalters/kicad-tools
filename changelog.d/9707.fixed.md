- **Silkscreen repair and reference placement no longer build zeroed `DesignRules` placeholders** (Issue #9707). `check_silk_hole_overlap` and `Pin1MarkerRule.check` ignore their design-rules argument (the hole-overlap threshold is the shared `SILK_HOLE_CLEARANCE_MM` constant and the pin-1 check is purely geometric), so both now accept a PCB-only call; `SilkScreenRepairer._pin1_marker_refs`, `SilkScreenRepairer._measure_hole_residual` and `references_over_holes` drop their `DesignRules(min_trace_width_mm=0.0, ...)` stand-ins and call the checks with the PCB alone. This keeps the manufacturer-propagation lint (`MFR001`) green on `main` without new allowlist entries.
