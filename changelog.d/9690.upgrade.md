- **`kct check` can newly flag copper inside filled Edge.Cuts shapes** (Issue #9690).
  Boards with a filled `gr_circle`/`gr_poly`/`gr_rect` cut-out (including a bare
  circle/rect with no fill token, which KiCad parses as filled) now fail
  `edge_clearance_*` for copper inside that region — matching kicad-cli, which
  such boards were already failing at the fab. Move the copper out of the
  cut-out, or mark the shape explicitly unfilled with `(fill no)` if it was
  never meant to be a cut-out.
