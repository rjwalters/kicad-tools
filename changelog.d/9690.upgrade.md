- **`kct check` can newly flag copper near or inside Edge.Cuts cut-out shapes**
  (Issue #9690). Boards with a filled `gr_circle`/`gr_poly`/`gr_rect` cut-out
  (including a bare circle/rect with no fill token, which KiCad parses as
  filled) now fail `edge_clearance_*` for copper inside that region, and any
  board whose Edge.Cuts carries a `gr_circle` or `gr_poly` now has that
  shape's boundary enforced as board edge — copper just outside or straddling
  it is flagged even for `(fill no)` shapes — all matching kicad-cli, which
  such boards were already failing at the fab. Move the copper out of the
  cut-out or away from the boundary, or move the shape off Edge.Cuts if it
  was never meant to be board edge.
