- **`kct fix-silkscreen --all`: one shared silk-legalisation pass** (Issue #9797, phase 1 of #9795). The new `legalize_silk(pcb, mfr_profile)` library entry point composes the existing width, height, pad-clearance and hole-clearance repairs with reference relocation planned against the repaired geometry, and reports every item it cannot make legal instead of waiving it. Vias are excluded from clipping and relocation per the #9795 operator ruling (silk over a via is left alone), text strokes thinner than the profile's minimum width are thickened even at legal height, and `DesignRules` gains an optional profile-supplied `min_silk_to_hole_clearance_mm` that falls back to the `silk_hole_overlap` threshold when omitted. Existing `fix-silkscreen` flags keep their behaviour.
