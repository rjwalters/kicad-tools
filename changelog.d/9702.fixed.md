- **Arc clearance now measures the true arc, not a 1-2 um-wide covering tessellation** (Issue #9702). `kct check`'s pairwise arc clearance verdict is an exact capsule measurement along the authored centreline (round end caps included), and arc pairs are compared against the arc acceptance band probed on pinned kicad-cli 10.0.6 (490 nm-short gaps pass, 500 nm-short ones flag -- all four orientations share one band). A gap at or above the requirement is never flagged, the reported `actual` is the real edge-to-edge distance (matching kicad-cli's own number), and `kct apply --checked` no longer refuses an arc placed exactly at the clearance: `kct probe`, checked apply and `kct check` on the saved board agree at the minimum. The netclass copper rule consumes the same measured band, and the probe's non-pairwise arc stand-in now carries the authored width at a 0.1 um sagitta, so it no longer reads 1 um per side wider than the saved board's own check.
