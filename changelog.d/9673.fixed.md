- **Copper arcs are no longer invisible to `kct check`'s pairwise clearance/short rules** (Issue #9673). Authored `(arc ...)` track copper is now collected beside segments, pads and vias, measured through the same 1 um-sagitta chord tessellation `kct probe` uses, and reported as the arc itself: a foreign-net segment crossing an applied arc's apex now reports `clearance_arc_segment` (`SHORT: ...`) instead of reading clean, and arc-vs-pad / arc-vs-via / arc-vs-arc pairs get `clearance_arc_pad` / `clearance_arc_via` / `clearance_arc_arc`. Findings name the arc by its geometry descriptor (`Arc@layer:w<width>:<start>~<mid>~<end>`), and the segment-endpoint via exemption applies at authored arc endpoints only. `kct probe`'s arc items keep their covering-tessellation check pass, so probe and post-`apply` `kct check` agree.
