- **Auto-pour no longer covers another pour net's pads with a bounding-box island** (Issue #6402). When `kct route`'s auto-pour gives a net a pad-bbox island on a layer that already hosts another net's zone (board 03's VBUS island on the VCC plane's `In2.Cu`), the island is now carved (with a straight notch where a hole would be needed) or shrunk to the net's own pads so the incumbent's pads, such as U1's VCC pin and C1, stay on their plane.
