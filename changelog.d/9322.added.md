- **Per-project CPL rotation corrections via `cpl_rotations.yaml`** (Issue #9322).
  A sidecar next to the `.kicad_pcb` declares `by_reference` / `by_lcsc` degrees
  that `kct export` and `kct readiness --generate` add to the KiCad rotation
  (precedence: reference, then LCSC, then the shipped footprint table), so
  pin headers and connectors the fab's library models rotate differently can be
  fixed without a footprint glob that would hit every other header. The sidecar
  is hashed into `readiness.json` (editing or deleting it stales the evidence and
  fails `--verify`), applied corrections are recorded under `cpl_rotations`, and
  polarised/asymmetric parts (`J`/`U`/`D`/`Q`/`LED`) that no correction matched
  are listed as `cpl_rotations.unreviewed` (a review list, not a blocker). Also
  removes the nonexistent `PnPExportConfig(rotation_offsets=...)` example from
  the manufacturing-export guide.
