- **`kct bom` / MCP `export_bom` no longer drop designators with `group_by="none"`** (Issue #9382). The JLCPCB and PCBWay formatters keyed their ungrouped branch on `(value, footprint[, lcsc])`, so identical parts collided and every designator but the last was silently omitted from the CSV. They now emit one row per item; grouped output is unchanged, and the Seeed/Generic formatters were never affected.
