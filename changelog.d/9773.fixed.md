- **`kct` exchanges the whole front/back padstack shape definition when flipping a one-sided custom pad** (Issue #9773). A `front_inner_back` padstack whose outer sides are not both custom (one side a plain shape with no `(primitives ...)` block) previously skipped the front/back exchange entirely, leaving the copper and stale shape metadata on the wrong layer. Measured against `pcbnew` 10.0.6 `Flip` on the CI-pinned image (golden fixture pads 8-11), KiCad moves the shape, size, custom options and primitives together; the shared transform used by both the recovery mirror and library back placement now does the same.
