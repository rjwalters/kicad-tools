- **`path_ampacity` no longer counts an in-pad sense tap seeded at the trunk's own off-centre landing** (Issue #9677). When no copper reaches the endpoint pad's centre, the resolver's fallback used to keep any buried segment under the route's landing point -- including a sense tap's first segment where the tap continues out of the pad -- so the resolved path inherited the tap's 0.3 mm neck and the check judged the tap at the full declared current. Wholly pad-internal copper that feeds an external edge leaving the pad where the declared route does not land is now excluded by pad connectivity, while copper that genuinely ends inside the pad, spans the route's own landings, or chains to the pad centre is still kept as path evidence.
