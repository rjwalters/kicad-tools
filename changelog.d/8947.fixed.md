- **`kct`'s strict Gerber reader accepts legal KiCad 10 copper layers it
  wrongly rejected** (Issue #8947). A zero-size aperture
  (`%ADD..C,0.000000*%`) is now rejected only when flashed or stroked —
  defining, selecting, and using it solely for `G36`/`G37` regions, KiCad's
  documented fill pattern, parses. Self-touching zone-fill region contours
  are repaired with `shapely.make_valid` under an area-preservation guard
  (a self-intersecting contour with ambiguous interior is still rejected).
  This unblocks `mask_to_copper` on `B_Cu` and inner copper layers of
  boards with zone fills; layers using aperture macros (`%AM`) remain
  unsupported.
