- **`kct route` says why a net was routed wider than its class width** (Issue #8564). Nets sized by impedance (declared target, or one synthesized from a validator default such as `.*CLK$`) now log one warning-level line with the width, target Ω and origin, and the route decision rationale carries the same reason. Also fixes route decisions never being recorded (an empty `DecisionStore` is falsy). Routed widths are unchanged.
