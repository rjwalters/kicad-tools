- **Arc-vs-arc clearance prunes chord pairs that cannot change the verdict** (Issue #9714). `kct check` on boards with large-radius copper arcs no longer pays the full chord-pair product between neighbouring arcs: a bounding-box lower bound skips exact distance evaluations that cannot lower the gap or move its witness, and an arc's chord list is memoised per element. On the issue's concentric 50 mm fixtures, 50 arcs drop from 13.5 s to ~1.1 s (12x) with 24x fewer exact kernel calls, 20 arcs from 2.0 s to 0.15 s (13x), and a 400-arc small-fillet control from 0.20 s to 0.09 s -- findings identical in gap, location, nets and items. Arc-vs-pad/via/zone pairs keep the exhaustive search.
