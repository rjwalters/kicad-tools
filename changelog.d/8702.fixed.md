- **Chained `--complete` pass no longer refused for advancing an open net**
  (Issue #8702). A pass that connected some, but not all, pads of a net it
  could not finish re-reported that net's connectivity error with new pad
  counts, and was refused as if its copper had added a DRC error. Connectivity
  errors are now compared by net; other error classes keep their full
  message-and-location key.
