- **`match_group_length_skew` can newly fail on boards with same-net loops or
  dead-end stubs above 4.0 mm** (Issue #9304). Copper the signal never travels no
  longer masks real skew in the measured figure, so a group whose defect copper
  was hiding an out-of-tolerance spread now reports the corrected (usually
  larger) skew. Fix the copper (remove the loop/stub and restore any needed
  length as real series length -- see board 07's `repair_byte0_loops_9118.py`
  for the pattern), not the tolerance: the gate is not allowlisted by group.
