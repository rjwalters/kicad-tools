- **`kct stitch` keeps stub tracks out of the same-net pad slit band** (Issue #9846). With `--avoid-pad-overlap`, every leg of the emitted 0.2 mm pad-to-via track is now held to the two-region gap predicate against same-net SMD pads on the trace's own layer — the same `pad_spacing` detection edge that already seats the via annulus (#8957): a leg touching/overlapping a pad is one copper union and a gap at/beyond the edge is not a finding, but a `(0, edge)` slit (board 06's U2.B4 0.125 mm and U3.48 0.100 mm GND stubs) is refused at the placement ladder, which walks on to a legal alternative, with a commit-time backstop that drops and reports the pad (`same_net_pad_gap` skip reason) if no strategy can seat it. Pads on other layers share no copper with the track and never reject it. Removes the GND half of the board 06 `physical_copper_gap` allowance (`check_diffpair_coverage.py`); the MIPI_CLK+ half stays (#9019).
