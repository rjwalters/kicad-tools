- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` re-centres tracks that enter their own pad off-centre** (Issue #9334). New repairer for `mask_to_copper` errors whose conductor is a straight same-net segment with one end inside its pad: that end moves to the pad centre. Included in the default set when the detection report carries `mask_to_copper` findings (`kct check` JSON or the pure-Python engine); `--only mask-copper` detects with `kct check`'s rules. Each saved move is re-checked and reverted if its finding persists or a new one names the segment or pad; vias, pours, arcs, pass-through, straddle, both-ends-inside and different-net findings are never touched and are listed with a reason.
