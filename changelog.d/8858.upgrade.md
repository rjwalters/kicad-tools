- **`mask_to_copper` errors can drop to info** (Issue #8858). Measurements whose
  exposure lies wholly on the owner pad's own copper now carry disposition
  `within_own_pad` (severity info, assessment `passed`). Consumers that read
  `mask_copper_assessments[].measurements[].disposition` must accept the new
  value. Error counts fall on boards that use a 0 mm pad mask expansion. No
  finding becomes newly severe. Same-net copper beside a pad is still an error
  at the 0.09 mm band.
