- **`mask_to_copper` errors can drop to info** (Issue #8858). Clean same-net pad
  entries now carry disposition `within_own_pad`, at severity info, and the
  assessment can pass with them. Consumers that read
  `mask_copper_assessments[].measurements[].disposition` must accept the new
  value. Info findings never fail `kct check`, including under `--strict`, which
  gates on errors and warnings only (verified). Error counts fall on boards
  routed with 0 mm pad mask expansion. No finding becomes more severe.
