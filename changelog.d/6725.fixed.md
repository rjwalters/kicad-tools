- **`kct check` and the skew tuners measure net length exactly as kicad-cli does** (Issue #6725). `diffpair_length_skew`, `match_group_length_skew` and the router's pair (#6664) and match-group (#6675) tuners now share one function, `router/kicad_length.py`. A via is charged KiCad's stackup height between the layers it actually connects (outer copper full, inner end copper half, solder mask excluded; from the board's `(setup (stackup))`, else KiCad's default for `(general (thickness))` and the copper layer count) instead of the manufacturer profile's full board thickness. Track chains entering a pad or via are clipped at the copper boundary and the in-copper part is replaced by a straight line to the centre. Per-net lengths agree with kicad-cli to about a micron, so a pair the tuner reports as matched no longer fails kicad-cli's `skew_out_of_range` (07rf DQS: 0.000 vs 0.052 mm). `board_thickness_mm` and `blind_buried_supported` no longer enter the measurement.
