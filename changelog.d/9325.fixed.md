- **`tht_smd_spacing` no longer flags same-net anchor-via pairs** (Issue #9325).
  `kct pcb reinforce` lays consecutive wire legs so their end anchor vias sit
  ~2.3-2.5 mm apart on one net; the rule paired those exposed annuli and
  reported one error per junction (7 of 11 on the reporter's board) where the
  fab's DFM counted none. Same-net anchor↔anchor pairs are now exempt. A
  same-net anchor against a fan-out-sized annulus (#8925 item 5), any
  different-net pair, and two anchor vias colliding on net 0 (both
  unconnected — net 0 is the no-net sentinel, not a shared net) are still
  reported.
