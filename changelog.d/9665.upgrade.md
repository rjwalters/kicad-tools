- **`kct voltage-map-merge` now fails on a within-scenario spelling collision**
  (Issue #9665). A scenario map that spells one net two ways (`/VCC` and `VCC`)
  is rejected with a `ValueError` naming both spellings; previously one of the
  two declared potentials was silently dropped from the envelope. If a merge
  starts failing with this diagnostic, keep one spelling per net within each
  scenario map — differing potentials for the same net belong in separate
  scenarios, which still merge onto one normalised key.
