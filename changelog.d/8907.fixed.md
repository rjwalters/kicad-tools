- **`kct bom` accepts `--check-availability`, `--validate`, `--quantity` and
  `--no-cache`** (Issue #8907). The four flags were implemented by the BOM
  command but declared only on the standalone `kicad-bom` parser, so the
  unified CLI rejected them with `unrecognized arguments`. They are now
  declared on the `kct bom` subparser, listed in `kct bom --help`, and
  forwarded with their values.
