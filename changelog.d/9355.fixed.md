- **Fold-back repair no longer declines a legal repair because of legs an
  earlier repair removed** (Issue #9355). The collision model `plan_foldback_repairs`
  checks against is a pre-repair snapshot, so once a repair on one net deleted its
  two legs, a later net on the same layer was still vetoed against them and
  reported unrepairable (`skipped`, `sharp_trace_corner`). The planner now asks
  `VectorCollisionChecker.ignoring()` to treat the legs it has already removed as
  absent; copper added by earlier repairs still vetoes via `clearance=`, and the
  raster fallback keeps its conservative snapshot.
