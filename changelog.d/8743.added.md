- **`kct pcb reinforce` opens the solder mask along each wire leg** (Issue #8743). In the default `ends` mode every fully anchored leg gets a board-level `gr_line` on its own side's mask layer from anchor to anchor (width = trace width, or `--mask-width MM`), and the anchors are written with per-via tenting off. `--no-mask-opening` / `--mask-opening` override; with `--voltage-map` an opening that would break the pairwise creepage requirement is refused and reported. Re-runs are idempotent.
