- **`redundant_copper_staircase` stops holding back legal shortcuts** (Issue
  #9848). Shortcut legs are now judged with the same clearance acceptance band
  as `netclass_clearance_copper` (an exact tie no longer rejects a leg on float
  noise), new copper in a same-net pad's mask zone is accepted when each
  proposed leg is one clean entry under `mask_to_copper`'s `within_own_pad`
  criteria, and a chain node that joins same-net copper only by overlap no
  longer splits the chain when the replacement copper keeps contact with every
  terminal it spans. Entries through a positive mask expansion, traces that
  merely pass a pad's zone, and candidates that would strand a via or pad keep
  their rejection.
