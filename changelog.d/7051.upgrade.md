- **`--batch-routing` is deprecated** (Issue #7051). Scripts passing it now get a warning on stderr (and a `DeprecationWarning`); remove the flag, routing output is identical. `Autorouter.route_all_negotiated(batch_routing=...)` is accepted but ignored. The `BatchPathfinder`, `BatchRouteRequest`, `BatchRouteResult`, `batch_heuristic_gpu` and `compute_batch_costs_gpu` import paths from `kicad_tools.acceleration.kernels` are gone. See #7053.
