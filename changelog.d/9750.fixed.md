- **`kct check` no longer errors on a same-net bar wider than its own pad, or on a track entering a custom pad** (Issue #9750). A straight segment whose copper covers the pad's whole perimeter band (a bus bar onto a through-hole lead) now reads `within_own_pad` (info) for round and square pads alike, instead of failing the one-crossing allowance meant for narrower tracks; and `pad:custom` owners (e.g. the stock SOT-89 tab) join the `within_own_pad` class with conditions measured against the pad's real copper outline. Chamfered, padstack and solder-mask-defined pads keep their measured verdict, and pass-through/straddling copper still errors.
