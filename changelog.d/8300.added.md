- **`kct optimize-traces` honours HV creepage with `--voltage-map`** (Issue #8300). New `--voltage-map`, `--creepage-standard`, `--pollution-degree`, `--material-group` and `--hv-threshold` flags (same semantics and defaults as `kct route`). Each candidate edit that would bring a net pair below its pairwise creepage requirement is rejected on its own while other edits on the net still apply; the text and `--format json` summaries report skipped edits by net pair. Without `--voltage-map` behaviour is unchanged.
