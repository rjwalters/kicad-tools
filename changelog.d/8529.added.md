- **`kct footprint generate rpi-hat`** (Issue #8529). Generates a Raspberry Pi HAT part: the 2x20 header (pin 1 at the origin) plus four unnumbered 2.7 mm NPTH mounting holes (58 x 49 mm), 6 mm footprint-local copper keep-outs (`--keepout-diameter`) and the 65 x 56.5 mm outline on F.Fab, so the holes cannot drift from the header. The `connector_edge_distance` inventory now also covers `J`-reference footprints outside `Connector_*` libraries (such composite parts were silently dropped), and `create_hat_regions()` defaults to the 65 x 56.5 mm HAT spec (was 56 mm).
