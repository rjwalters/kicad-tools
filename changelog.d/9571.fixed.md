- **`kct pcb sync-netlist` adds new label nets with the board's `/` convention on
  auto-named boards** (Issue #9571). The dominant-convention census excluded
  KiCad's `unconnected-(...)` nets but not its other auto-generated name class,
  `Net-(R1-Pad2)`, so boards whose remaining nets are auto-named read as
  bare-dominant and a new root-sheet label net was added bare on a board whose
  label nets are `/A`, `/B`, ... Auto-named nets are now excluded alongside
  power rails and ground; a board of only auto-named nets has no convention and
  still resolves to the historical bare default (#3370).
