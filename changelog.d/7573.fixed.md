- **Plane-via reservation now covers pour-only 2-pad parts** (Issue #7573).
  `select_plane_via_pads` serves a footprint with fewer than 3 pads when every
  pad is on a pour net (e.g. a +5V/GND decoupling cap), so signal copper can no
  longer take its only stitch sites. Signal-carrying 2-pad parts stay skipped.
