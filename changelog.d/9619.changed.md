- **`kct check` copper-clearance verdicts now use KiCad's acceptance band** (Issue #9619). A new `KICAD_CLEARANCE_BAND_MM` (5e-4 mm / 0.5 um, applied by the new `drc_clear()` predicate) governs only the probed copper-clearance verdict sites (`clearance.py`, `diffpair_clearance_intra.py`, `netclass_clearance_copper.py`): it is the largest shortfall pinned KiCad 10.0.6 accepts on the black-box probe (10 nm steps refined to 1 nm: a 500 nm shortfall is accepted, 501 nm flagged, on parallel tracks, a 45-degree pair and a pad-to-track pair alike; pinned in `tests/test_clearance_slack_kicad_band_9619.py`). Marginal clearance gaps of 100-500 nm that `kct check` used to report as failing are now accepted on those rules, matching `kicad-cli drc`; shortfalls beyond 500 nm still fail. `DRC_TOLERANCE` stays 1e-4 mm for every other check -- dimensions (track width, via drill/diameter, annular ring), netclass floors, hole-to-copper, silk, via-in-pad, via-under-body, and the `fabrication_process` eligibility twin: the review probe showed pinned kicad-cli flags a 100 nm track-width or drill shortfall outright (no band), so widening those to 500 nm would let `kct check` pass boards `kicad-cli` rejects (regression tests pin the 400-600 nm rows). Everything that *writes* copper is likewise unchanged at 100 nm (the new `WRITER_CLEARANCE_MARGIN_MM`): the router's commit gates, `router/io.py`, the lattice and the C++ `clearance_kernel.hpp`, `kct pcb move-via` and `kct fix-vias`' via relocations, and the placement optimiser's incremental DRC all keep their own 1e-4 margin so copper kct writes stays a margin inside KiCad's tolerance -- rounding on save cannot tip it into a `kicad-cli` violation (a documented split, not a parity bug). Creepage verdicts also keep 1e-4: the probe measured kicad-cli copper clearance, not the IEC 60664-1 creepage requirement. The clearance band edge gains a 1e-9 mm float guard so an exactly-at-the-band gap is decided by KiCad's integer-nanometre semantics, not board-file float dust. The trade-off: on fabs whose own DRC is stricter than KiCad's, a 100-500 nm clearance shortfall can now pass kct's clearance checks while the fab's CAM would reject it -- KiCad parity was the explicit goal (#9619), and the opt-in `physical_copper_gap` fab-view pass still flags sub-floor gaps against the fab's own table.
