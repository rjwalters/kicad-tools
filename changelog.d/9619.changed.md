- **`kct check` clearance comparisons now use KiCad's acceptance band** (Issue #9619). The shared clearance slack (`CLEARANCE_EPSILON_MM`, `DRC_TOLERANCE`, the pairwise/creepage PASS tolerances and the router commit gates) moves from 1e-4 mm (0.1 um) to 5e-4 mm (0.5 um) -- the largest shortfall pinned KiCad 10.0.6 accepts on the black-box track-to-track probe (500 nm accepted, 600 nm flagged). Marginal gaps of 100-500 nm that `kct check` used to report as failing (and `kct route` used to reject) are now accepted, matching `kicad-cli drc`; shortfalls beyond 500 nm still fail. The trade-off: on fabs whose own DRC is stricter than KiCad's, a 100-500 nm shortfall can now pass kct's checks while the fab's CAM would reject it -- KiCad parity was the explicit goal (#9619), and a stricter per-fab floor remains a follow-up.
