- **`diffpair_uncoupled_length` now equals kicad-cli's `diff_pair_uncoupled` figure** (Issue #6554). kct paired every parallel overlap, so on real copper it read lower than KiCad (board 03 `USB_D` 8.130 mm against KiCad's 10.405 mm; board 06 LVDS 1.216 mm against 1.541 mm). It now reproduces KiCad's primitive pairing: pairs are summed, and a pair does not couple when a track, via or pad reaches the probe KiCad runs between the two traces (at the start of the overlap along the P trace, in KiCad's integer nanometres), unless that track or via touches an end of the overlap or one of the traces leaves that pad, so a stub buried in its own pad is not paired. The figure matches kicad-cli within 1 um on the committed boards, on all nine pairs of board 06's regression fixture and on the pinned probe set.
