- **Post-route passes can no longer silently break a diff pair's skew**
  (Issue #6447). A shared guard (`router/pair_postpass_invariant.py`) measures
  every pair's `kct check` skew before and after the DRC nudge pass (including
  its via-in-pad sweep) and the via-hairpin collapse. If a pass pushes a pair
  past its error-severity skew limit, or more than 0.01 mm further past it, that
  pass's edits to the pair are reverted and logged as a `[pair-skew guard]`
  line. `kct retune-lengths` refuses (`pair_skew_regression`, nothing written)
  a retune that does the same to any pair on the board. This would have caught
  the #7784 board 07 DQS regression (0.119 -> 1.291 mm).
