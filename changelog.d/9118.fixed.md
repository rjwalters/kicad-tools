- **Board 07's `SDRAM_BYTE0` is matched on its real pad-to-pad paths** (Issue #9118).
  DQ7 and LDQM carried same-net loops through their own pads, so the real
  byte-0 spread was 7.5 mm against the 5 mm `group_skew_mm` while `validate.py`
  reported 0.18 mm. `real_design/repair_byte0_loops_9118.py` removes the loops
  and restores the length as `SerpentineGenerator` trombones; the physical
  spread is now 0.92 mm. No tolerance changes. `real_design/physical_path.py`
  measures the shortest path through the copper, and a regression test fails on
  the pre-repair copper. The published `output/` release is not re-issued.
