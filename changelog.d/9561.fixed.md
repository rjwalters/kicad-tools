- **`path_ampacity` counts off-centreline tie vias in a layer-change bar** (Issue #9561).
  The layer-change rating only admitted vias whose centre sat exactly on the resolved
  centreline of both layers, so hand-placed stitching vias inside a wide two-layer bar
  never raised the reported via count or capacity and the warning could not be cleared
  by stitching. A same-net via whose pad overlaps continuous path copper on both joined
  layers and chains to a counted via within 3.5 mm is now counted as a parallel tie via
  (capacity summed), the finding breaks the count down ("1 on the centreline, 7 tie vias
  in the bar"), and nearby same-net vias it rejects are named with a reason (off the bar
  copper, not spanning both layers, beyond the tie bound). Vias in series and
  stitching-in-doubled-copper behaviour (#8916) are unchanged.
