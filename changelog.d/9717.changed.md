- **`kct route --complete --voltage-map` names its attach-zone policy** (Issue #9717). The post-route HV audit applies the #4506 rated-footprint attach zones unconditionally, while `kct creepage` grants that exemption only with `--waive-attach-zone` — so a board the route gate passed could fail a census the designer ran without the flag. The completion result now prints `Attach-zone exemption: ENABLED` (zone count, and the census replay command carrying `--waive-attach-zone`) whenever the voltage-map audit consulted attach zones, and omits it entirely without a voltage map. The census default is unchanged and still stricter: copper outside the zones keeps failing even with the flag enabled.
