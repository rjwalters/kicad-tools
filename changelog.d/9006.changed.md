- **`kct readiness` hashes the waivers sidecar into evidence** (Issue #9006).
  The `<board>.kct-waivers.json` (or legacy `.kct_waivers.json`) sidecar that
  `kct check` auto-loads is now listed in `readiness.json` `inputs` with a
  sha256, mirroring `net_class_map.json`; editing a waiver after evidence is
  written now stales the evidence, and evidence that omits a present sidecar
  is refused as "omits current project or rule files".
