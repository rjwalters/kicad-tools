- **Connectivity checks can newly report opens and shorts** (Issues #6452,
  #6453, #6470). `validate()` / `kct validate --connectivity` no longer treats
  a pad inside a zone's outline as connected unless its copper touches the
  fill. `NetStatus.open_connections` and `validate()` now count stranded
  track/via fragments and lone fill islands, as kicad-cli does; the
  pad-based `island_count` and `routed_connections` are unchanged, and the
  new `NetStatus.padless_components` holds the difference. A blind via no longer
  bonds a pad on a layer it does not span. Copper LVS can report pad
  overlaps and track-on-pad contacts as shorts that the eroded pad box used
  to miss. Every new finding is one kicad-cli also reports.
