- **Match-group skew no longer counts a member net's material off-path copper** (Issue #9138). When `derive_group_skew_data` finds more than 2 mm of off-path copper on a member net (a same-net dead-end loop, overlapping duplicate or stub), it measures that net along its pad-to-pad copper path plus the height of the vias on it, so the loop can no longer hide real skew, and logs the excluded amount as a warning. Nets within the 2 mm margin — where the residual is barrel overlap under pads/vias and in-pad joints (#9117) — and nets with no pad-to-pad path (open nets, no shapely) keep the summed kicad-cli length, unchanged from before.
