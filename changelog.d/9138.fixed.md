- **`kct check` reports off-path copper on match-group member nets** (Issue #9138). When `derive_group_skew_data` finds more than 4.0 mm of same-net copper on no pad-to-pad path, it logs a warning naming the group, the net and the amount, so copper that inflates a member's length and can mask real skew is visible instead of silent; the threshold sits above the benign residual this repo's reference boards carry (up to ~3.4 mm of tuner length-matching cycle copper and barrel overlap under pads/vias), so what is reported is the anomalous amounts rather than most members. The derived skew figure itself is unchanged: it keeps the summed kicad-cli length for every member, the same ruler the router's match-group tuner length-matches against (shedding off-path copper per member was measured to manufacture skew — see the follow-up from Issue #9138 for the evidence and the open design).
