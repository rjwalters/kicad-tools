- **`kct check --mfr`'s mask-to-copper pass no longer scans every
  opening against every conductor** (Issue #8901). The attributed
  mask/copper comparison prefilters candidate pairs with a bounding-box
  index, so a board whose check was dominated by that sweep gets the
  same assessment far faster: on `boards/03-usb-joystick` the pass drops
  from 170.4 s to 12.9 s and the whole check from 191.6 s to 34.9 s,
  with identical findings, measurements and `evaluated_pairs`.
