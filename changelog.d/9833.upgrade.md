- **`kct sch connections` / `sch pin-map` / `sch find-unconnected` may newly report open nets** (Issue #9833). Schematics that relied on a T-connection without a junction dot, a pin on a wire's middle, or a power symbol on a wire's middle were reported as connected before; the KiCad netlist leaves all three open, and the tools now agree with it. If such a connection is intended, add a junction dot at the T (or move the pin/power symbol to the wire's endpoint); `sch connections` prints a warning naming each affected coordinate. Boards regenerated from kct generators are unaffected — the active board recipes already emit junction-dotted or endpoint-meeting wiring.
