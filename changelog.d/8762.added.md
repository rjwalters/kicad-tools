- **`kct readiness --generate --merge-lcsc-from PATH`** (Issue #8762). Merges verified LCSC numbers from a previous BOM CSV into the generated bundle, as `kct export --merge-lcsc-from` does, so a board with a seed BOM can reach READY through `--generate`. The source is recorded as `metrics.lcsc_merge_source`; an unresolved-BOM blocker now says when no merge source was supplied. Rejected with `--verify` or a missing file.
