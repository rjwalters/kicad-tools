- **`kct check` `pad_grid` verdict on a part no longer depends on other parts** (Issue #9434). The auto-derived tolerance was a percentile of the whole board's pad offsets, so moving a few fiducials onto round coordinates could start flagging parts that had not moved. It is now derived per footprint from that footprint's own pad lattice (same 0.05 mm floor / 0.15 mm cap, same stock-library allowance from #3061); `--pad-grid-tolerance` and `--pad-grid-strict` are unchanged.
