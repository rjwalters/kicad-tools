- **`kct route --group-length-window <pct>` groups similar-length nets without a shared name** (Issue #9621). Multi-pad signal nets whose MST lengths fall within `pct` percent of a cluster's shortest member become synthetic `LEN<n>` bus groups, routed through the existing `--bus-routing` machinery (`--strategy basic`) with members ordered by escape feasibility (fewest open pad directions first). The log names each group and its members. Off by default; `--strategy negotiated` and the escalation ladders now print an "ignored" warning for it and for `--bus-routing`. On the synthetic congested rows tried, grouping did not change completion versus the default order (measured refutation recorded in `tests/router/test_group_length_window.py`).
