- **`mask_to_copper` reinforce attribution net-gates pad openings** (Issue #9348). A pad opening within the strip tolerance of a run's anchor is now attributed to that run only when the pad carries the run's net, as anchor vias already were; a different-net pad no longer reports the run's `owner_net`/`owner_run`, nor can its opening excuse run-net copper as `within_reinforce_run`.
