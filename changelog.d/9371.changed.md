- **`Changelog Fragment Check` verdicts are computed from the PR's live
  labels** (Issue #9371). The workflow now evaluates via
  `gh pr view` (`--pr N --repo OWNER/NAME`) instead of the triggering
  event's payload, so two rapid `changelog:skip` flips whose runs finish out
  of order can no longer leave the required check reporting a stale verdict;
  a run's verdict reflects the label state at its read.
