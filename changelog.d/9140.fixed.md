- **Coupled diff-pair legs keep out of the own-net pad slit band** (Issue #9140). The coupled engine's pad gate (`_span_pad_clear`) now applies the same two-region rule as the single-net A* (#9019): pose/trunk legs and synthesized stub tails may union their own SMD pad (gap <= 0) or stay at or beyond the resolved `same_net_pad_gap_band_edge_mm`; gaps in between (e.g. 0.1275 mm < 0.15 mm) are rejected. The joint-search candidate (Python A* or the C++ joint path) and the Phase-B fine-grid repair are swept through the same rule before commit, so a pair whose only coupled path crosses the band falls back to the guarded single-net router instead of committing the slit. Off when no edge is resolved; foreign-pad logic is unchanged.
