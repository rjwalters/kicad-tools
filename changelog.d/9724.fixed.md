- **`kct check` copper-to-edge verdicts now use KiCad's acceptance band** (Issue #9724). The #9619 probe's follow-up leg measured `copper_edge_clearance` on pinned KiCad 10.0.6 (straight rectangular edge, 0.5 mm `min_copper_edge_clearance`, 10 nm steps refined to 1 nm): a 500 nm shortfall is accepted and 501 nm flagged -- the same cut-off as copper clearance -- so `edge.py`'s copper-to-edge comparisons (traces, SMD pads, zone copper) now ride `drc_clear()`/`KICAD_CLEARANCE_BAND_MM` instead of the 1e-4 float epsilon. Copper-to-edge findings 100-500 nm below the requirement that `kct check` used to report are now accepted, matching `kicad-cli pcb drc` verdict-for-verdict (the 500/501 nm boundary is pinned black-box in `tests/test_clearance_slack_kicad_band_9619.py`). Via and through-hole-pad verdicts against `min_hole_to_edge_mm` are unchanged at the 1e-4 epsilon: the copper-edge probe cannot establish a tolerance for a separate hole minimum, so those branches keep flagging any shortfall beyond 0.1 um.
