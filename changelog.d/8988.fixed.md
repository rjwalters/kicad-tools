- **Hidden power-input pins name their net, as in KiCad** (Issue #8988). A legacy power symbol whose library definition lacks the `(power)` flag (KiCad's `pic_programmer` demo `VPP`) names its net through a hidden `power_in` pin; schematic connectivity now treats that pin as a global net of the pin's name, so `kct sync --analyze` no longer reports its pads as `Net-(#PWR...)` mismatches with stranded copper.
