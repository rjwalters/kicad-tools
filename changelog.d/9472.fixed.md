- **`kct pcb add-fiducials --sides populated` skips faces with only through-hole parts** (Issue #9472). "Populated" previously meant "carries any BOM footprint", so a back face holding just a flipped through-hole connector got three fiducials that nothing on that face needs. It now means "carries at least one SMD BOM part" (`(attr smd)` or any SMD pad). The `fiducials_missing` and `board_id_missing` rules share the definition, so they no longer flag a through-hole-only face either; a board with no SMD part has no populated side, and `--sides populated` exits with the existing "no populated side found" error.
