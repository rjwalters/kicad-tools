- **`kct route --preserve-existing` holds new copper to the kept copper's wider net class** (Issue #8577). A net whose copper was kept is skipped by the router, so a `--net-class-map` class merged after loading never reached that copper: a new Signal via could sit 0.183 mm from a kept Analog (0.2 mm) track, and a Signal track 0.224 mm from a kept Clock (0.25 mm) pad. kicad-cli DRC failed the output while the router's own census passed it. The class floor now reaches the kept copper and its pads, so the search, the native validator and the end-of-route census all use the larger of the two classes.
