- **Lattice diff-pair gateways keep the shortest workable combination
  and refuse polarity crossings** (Issue #7362). The fan-via gateway
  retry used to accept the first combination that routed, ranked only by
  fan length. It now routes up to 4 workable combinations per group
  (`against`, then pass-under) and keeps the shortest by P+N route
  length, with ties going to the earlier try. A combination that puts P on
  opposite sides of the segment at its two ends is rejected before it is
  routed: the coupled body could only reach it through a half loop. Board
  03's fresh USB_D route (seed 42, `jlcpcb-tier1`) goes from 18.32/19.80 mm
  back to 15.66/15.85 mm, inside the 16 mm bound with the #7324 90 ohm
  width kept. The `J1.A6->U2.3` leg falls back to uncoupled again, as it
  did before #7324. The gateway log line now reports `picked #k of n
  workable (r routed, p pruned)` and per-net lengths.
