- **Escape in-pad via nudge stays on a rotated pad's own copper**
  (Issue #9319). `EscapeRouter._select_in_pad_via_position` slid the
  clearance nudge along a board grid axis while deriving the
  stencil-safety budget from the pad's pre-rotation local dims, so a
  non-cardinal oblong pad (e.g. a 1.4 x 0.3 mm finger at 30/45 deg)
  could get its via drill parked entirely off its own copper — the
  opposite of the stencil-safety contract the routine documents. The
  slide now follows the pad's board-space long axis
  (`R(-rotation)`-rotated local long axis, the same convention
  `io.py:_pad_copper_polygon` uses) with the containment budget kept
  in the pad-local frame; cardinal-angle pads snap to the exact grid
  axes, so axis-aligned results are unchanged.
