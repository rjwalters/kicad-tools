- **`redundant_copper_staircase` only suggests legal shortcuts** (Issue #9817).
  The fewer-bend path is now cleared at the net-class pair requirement
  `netclass_clearance_copper` enforces (the larger of the two nets' classes,
  not just the fab floor), may put no new copper in a pad's mask-opening zone
  (the opening grown by the active `mask_to_copper` policy clearance), and a
  staircase chain now ends at same-net pad copper and via centres instead of
  walking through them. Routes that are already the minimum legal route are no
  longer flagged; genuine staircases with a legal shortcut still are.
