- **`kct optimize-placement` spaces bipolar and swinging HV pairs at their full
  voltage difference** (Issue #9710). `--voltage-map` collapsed every net to
  `max(|lo|, |hi|)` and `--hv-domains` took `abs()` of each declared voltage, so
  a `+150 V` / `-150 V` pair, or two nets each swinging `[-150, +150]`, reached
  the creepage keepout as a 0 V pair and got no spacing at all. Domains are
  still chosen by largest magnitude, but the required spacing is now looked up
  at the signed, end-to-end interval difference the `kct creepage` census uses
  (300 V -> 3.2 mm at PD2 / IIIa in that example). Required spacing can only
  rise; all-positive scalar maps are unchanged.
