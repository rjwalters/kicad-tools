- **Router no longer drops vias in the mask-dam zone beside SMD pads; boards 02, 05, 06 cleared of `via_at_pad_edge`** (Issue #7296). New
  `DesignRules.min_via_pad_edge_gap` (defaults to the manufacturer's `min_solder_mask_dam_mm`) makes the coupled
  diff-pair search refuse a via whose drill edge is closer than that to an SMD pad's copper, in both the Python and
  C++ (`ROUTER_CPP_BUILD_VERSION` 61) pathfinders, even where the fab allows via-in-pad. New `relocate_pad_edge_vias`
  slides existing offenders clear; it moved 2 vias on board 02, 3 on board 05 and 17 on board 06 (0 `via_at_pad_edge`
  left), with evidence re-pinned.
