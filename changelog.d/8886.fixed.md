- **Reinforcement checks no longer skip real wire runs on doubled-copper nets** (Issue #8886). `reinforcement_incomplete` and `wire_run_courtyard_overlap` now locate the wire of an ambiguous or unresolved `reinforcement_eligible` path from its solder-mask strip and anchor holes and check it. A declared path with no wire yields one `no_wire` finding; `[skipped]` findings are located at the path's source pad in rounded sheet coordinates.
