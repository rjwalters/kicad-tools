- **`kct route` / `kct pcb` difference voltage-map ranges end-to-end** (Issue #9602).
  A range-bearing `--voltage-map` (e.g. a `kct voltage-map-merge` envelope) collapsed
  each net to its dominant endpoint before differencing, so a sign-flipping net and its
  opposite bank scored 0 V apart and vanished from the route widening matrix entirely --
  while the `kct creepage` census saw 300 V. The route/link/reinforce tables now carry
  the full `[lo, hi]` intervals and use the census' worst-case endpoint combination
  `max(|a.hi - b.lo|, |b.hi - a.lo|)` (`_pair_bounds` caps still only lower); scalar-only
  maps are unchanged. Route-time spacing now matches the interval-aware census on the
  #9582 motivating topology (`LINE` vs follower bank at 3.2 mm).
