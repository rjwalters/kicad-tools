- **Grid router keeps own-net legs and the neck-down taper out of the fab pad-spacing slit band** (Issue #9019). With the stackup's `pad_spacing` detection edge resolved, the Python and C++ grid A* refuse a step whose copper parks in `(0, edge)` beside an own-net SMD pad (union with the pad or clear of the edge, the #9012 two-region rule), and the #1018 taper no longer narrows a leg into that band. Pads the leg is unioned with through the path (its own start/end pads) are exempt, so a plain pad-to-pad connection stays planar and via-free instead of escaping through a pair of pad-edge vias. The gate is a preference, not a hard rule: a net whose gated search finds no path is retried once with the gate off — only when the gate actually refused a step of that search, with the fallback counted and logged — so it never costs a connection. The native extension `BUILD_VERSION` is now 72 (`uv run kct build-native`). Board 02's seed-42 route is unchanged from the pre-gate baseline (12/12 nets, 29 vias, no `physical_copper_gap` findings). A taper piece that no width can lift out of the band (it ends just short of the pad its leg joins, so its end cap faces the pad across a slit) is now fused with its straight-run neighbours, at their narrowest width, into one segment that reaches the pad. Copper never gets wider than the taper profile. Both backends' route conversions do this, and the native per-cell segments are fused across cells. On board 06's seed-42 CI fixture this clears the MIPI_CLK+ slit at U4.1 (0.142 mm), so two of the original 13 pad-to-track gaps remain, both beside GND pads (U2.B4 and U3.48) and both added by the recipe's stitcher (#9846). The fixture's `mask_to_copper` errors also drop from 197 to 156; reach stays 21/21, vias stay at 175, and kicad-cli DRC reports 0 errors.
