  offsetting** (Issue #7931). `trim_end_reversals` now also cuts a jog- or
  45-degree-detour-and-return at either end of a fat centerline back to the
  straight step along the searched edge, when the loop stays inside the
  pair's own copper band (half pitch plus half trace width). The anchored end
  vertex never moves, and the trimmed centerline is re-verified like any
